## Description

Whilst deployed on exercise in the US, the AV was in the process of being recovered but an un-commanded increase in altitude occurred necessitating utilisation of operator manual control and Land Immediate command.  No damage to the AV. 
 
The aircraft was directed to IP1, which was executed as planned. The aircraft then proceeded to IP2/3, where it was expected to descend to the required altitude before continuing to IP4. However, during the IP2/3 phase, it was observed that the aircraft remained in the IP2/3 orbit for an extended period and was increasing in altitude rather than descending as intended.
 
In response, a waypoint was created at 400 feet, and a positive command was issued to lower the altitude. Despite this, the aircraft maintained its altitude, disregarding the mission update. Consequently, manual control (U-drive) was assumed to lower the aircraft locally while maintaining visual contact.
The Land Immediate command was enacted as a result of the main battery level having decreased to 1% by this point.
Upon recovery, the aircraft was found to be undamaged and required no maintenance
