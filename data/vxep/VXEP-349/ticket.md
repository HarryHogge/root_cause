## Description

Here was status from last month. I just poked them again. Our BD guys have been talking with Trillium to make sure they will be ready. I believe they should have flow it on their GA manned aircraft and then we will fly it Jan next year.
 
On Stalker, we need to resolve how to land with their payload sticking out. If we have to, then we can land on an air mattress which we have done before. A better approach would be a new landing system, like landing legs.
 
 
Here is the HD55 LD status information from Trillium:
 
- HD55-MVS-LD-LRF-LP prototype gimbal completed – current weight 2700grams
- MWIR, SWIR, and EO Cameras
- 55mJ 1064nm Laser Designator
- LRF
- 850nm LP
- Ground based laser testing ongoing
- DESB (Directed Energy Safety Board) brief - 10/27/2022
- First Flight Test – 11/14/2022
- LHE (Laser Hazard Evaluation) – December 2022
- Second Flight Test – January 2023
- DESB – March 2023
