## Issue Status

The following MAGNSS Prototype issues require resolution and constitute HIGH risk for the MARSOC MADNSS Retrofit:
- Payloads Interoperability
- Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) - There is a high probability that the EMI issues encountered with the protos will affect EO/IR gimbal performance and/or the gimbal will introduce additional EMI effects to the operation of MAGNSS payload.
- Power characterization - Gimbal transient and steady-state power load characteristics have not been quantified with the VXE MAGNSS proto aircraft system which could result in insufficient power margins on the aircraft power bus.
- Payloads Weight [Note: MARSOC requires MWIR (HD55) vs LWIR (HD40)]
- The MAGNSS proto with RGM installed cannot support the weight of Gimbal payloads E140 MWIR or Trillium HD55 (MARSOC)
- MAGNSS proto aircraft weight with RGM & batteries (no gimbal) = 46.26lb (3.74lb margin)
- E140 weight w/out mount= 4.4lb
- HD55 weight w/mount = 5.21lb
- HD40 weight w/mount (for reference only) = 3.12lb
- RGM fitment
- Retract Assembly Modifications
- Retract handle requires redesign to be compatible with GPS puck and RGM
- New extruded RGM design would require new upper and lower housings (fairings) to be designed and fabricated with new composite tooling
- New extruded RGM design would likely drive change to internal board stack, wire routing/lengths,etc.
- Retract assy (presumably from MARSOC) needs to be reassembled with new top plate
- Technical Risks
- Low Flight Hours
- The 4 prototypes delivered to LM have not yet been flown and we can expect issues to arise
- RGM anti-spoof capabilities have not been validated
- Current design issues being investigated
- The Avionics enclosure internal wires with Harwin connectors are posing issues for PreMagness production
- With prolonged vibration/temperature cycling (numerous flights) the MAGNSS proto aircraft may exhibit issues that will affect mission capability (MICAP)
- Changes to current MAGNSS 6.3.4 software required – ECD Oct 2024
- Schedule Risks
- Date GFX (MARSOC VXE30 aircraft) will be available
- Procurement – we will be supplying our only DEV RGM which needs to be re-packaged into spare RGM housing
- No current inventory of MAGNSS material to fulfill > qty 1 MARSOC demand
- Long-lead material procurement - RGM DACU, CRPA, etc
- Estimated 2–3-month delay of TIQUILA 40/40 Production Program
