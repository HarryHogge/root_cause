## Description

operator statement: For the 2nd flight of the night for system 895 we launched at approximately 2309hrs with no issues seen on the system, at approximately 0144hrs while the aircraft transitioned from fixed wing flight to VTOL motors it repositioned and was in the rough area of where the LZ was located, at this time it began a rapid decent with VTOL motors engaged failing to pause at the preprogrammed altitudes. The aircraft was not able to be paused, and it made a hard landing into a grassy area, while it was on the ground, the VTOL motors kept spinning rapidly until the cut motors command was given. Jowone said that during the VTOL initial decent the energy was at 60% and then instantly changed to 30% and showed the color red, he had no control to pause. 
Attached is the video of the landing as well as pictures obtained after the hard landing and the following day after I inspected the aircraft. The left wing shows some damage and fraying, there is no other visible damage. I also took a close look at the fuselage and did not see any loose or damaged components. I did not take anything apart as I am not certified or trained to do so. The HLB battery that was in the aircraft has been marked and labeled. GCS 258 was the one that was used.
