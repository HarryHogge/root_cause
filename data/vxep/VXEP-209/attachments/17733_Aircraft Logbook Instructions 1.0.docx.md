Aircraft Logbook Instructions

## Aircraft Configuration Log

## Power Source- Must be filled out and initialed by both the individual performing the configuration change and a second qualified Technician to ensure quality control.  Complete Modification log any time the aircraft is swapped between Main Battery and Fuel Cell configurations.

## Payload- Any integration of payloads onto the aircraft requires a Modification Log entry and dual signatures for quality assurance. Detail And list all changes performed to the aircraft.

## Gimbal- This refers to sensors that are placed into the sensor bay of the fuselage. Any operations involving a new sensor will require information to be logged into appropriate fields.

## Weight and Balance- If any of the above items listed are changed to the aircraft, start a new column, and record the weight and CG of the aircraft. Units will be measured to the nearest tenth.

## Aircraft Autopilot Settings

## All- Released software will need to be installed on the GCS. After successfully uploading new software, record the information as outlined in the blank fields. Further instructions are provided on how to locate this information, below.

## Fixed Wing Autopilot FW, Fixed Wing Autopilot S/N, Fixed Wing Checksum, XCOMM FW, XCOMM Checksum, VTOL Autopilot FW, VTOL Autopilot Checksum can all be located on the Help tab > About > About GCS window.

## XCOMM S/N and VTOL Autopilot S/N can be located using the IP Adress Calculator tool. ## Supplemental Documentation

## Service Bulletins (SB’s)– Upon receiving new SB’s, the Technician shall comply with all applicable guidance and instruction to maintain airworthiness of the aircraft and fill out the applicable fields. Place a copy of the SB in the back of the logbook and fill out the Modification Log.

## Airworthiness Directive (AD’s) – New AD’s shall be logged and initialed by every operator that is flying the affiliated aircraft. Place a copy of the AD in the back of the logbook for crew awareness and fill out Modification Log.

## Aircraft Radio Settings

## All- Must be filled out and initialed any time a setting is changed in-flight or on ground.
