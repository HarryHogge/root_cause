## Mishap Description

The mishap occurred while performing SCO (system checkout flights) for vehicle 604. The first 2 flights were done with no issues observed. During the 3rd flight after completing a full flight cycle a landing pause was performed (as part of the 3rd flight check). During this landing pause the vehicle was nudged left and right repeatedly and after a few nudge inputs the vehicle stopped responding to the controls, started an uncontrolled flyaway (at 10-15ft AGL), and crashed into the ground.

Controls seemed to be lost at around 1:19 of the landing video, nudging it in any other direction didn’t have a response.

NCR 2371 is associated with this.
