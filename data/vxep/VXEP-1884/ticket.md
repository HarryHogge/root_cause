## Description

During the flight, while transiting to a mission waypoint, OGCS displayed a low battery RTB warning and the aircraft began flying to IP1. We still had somewhere between 70 and 80% perecntage remaining. After commanding the aircraft to go back to the mission it began flying and displayed the same warning and behavior again. Jack commanded it back to the mission and we turned off auto low battery RTB. Roughly 30-45 min after getting established the aircraft gave another low battery RTB warning and began transiting to IP1 even after disabling the auto low battery RTB setting.
Also during flight we were having pretty significant altitude fluctuations of greater than 200'. We did receive a warning saying the aircraft was unable to maintain its commanded altitude. We commanded RTB with roughly 33% left in the main out of precaution. 
Lastly, during landing, the aircraft did not pause decent at around 10' like it normally does. It was not descending rapidly but did slightly hit the ground and go back approximately 5' and continue hovering. Jack commanding cut and safe motors to prevent the aircraft from continuing its hover and drift from the PLZ location.
