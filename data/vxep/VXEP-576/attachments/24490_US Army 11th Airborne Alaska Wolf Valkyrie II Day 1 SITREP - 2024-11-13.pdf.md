SITREP: WOLF VALKRIE II, DAY 1  
Summary/Key Points 
 
 Primary focus of the day was locating and preparing equipment at the TOC. We 
tried to figure out how to mount the SGCS2 on the roof to improve line of sight.  
After attempting to climb up on the snow-covered roof, we decided it was a best 
to not die on day 1 and tie strap the SGCS2 sector to a tall step ladder tomorrow.   
 1st Battalion 24th Infantry Regiment didn’t really coordinate well with us today. 
No clear instructions so far and several folks we talked to didn’t seem to know 
either. Still don’t have PowerPoint from SSG Hodge after several requests. 
 S GT Bouchard assigned to us and spent time learning about the system.  Very 
excited about fuel cell and use of propane was very attractive. They take propane 
everywhere. He liked that only four tanks would be needed to provide one week’s 
worth of 24/7 missions, assuming 6 hour flights.  
 RT Robotics (Vietnamese company) attending event. https://rtrobotics.com/ 
Founded by a Canadian SOF guy.  Flying Hera, the world’s only backpackable 
quadcopter capable of carrying 33 lbs (15kg) of payload.  No flights today.   
 VXE500 failed mag checks and required two mag cals to get it to finally pass the 
launch wizard. The fixed wing magnetometer is very fussy in this bird (same thing 
happened in Finland). 
 I nstalled fuel cell in VXE499 and performed mag checks – no calibration required 
 Performed frequency scans with Silvus – a large swath was clear in S-band – 
hopefully that holds true for the rest of the event! 
 Conducted ground testing of 2023 heated pitot prototype updated with current 
limiting and switch.  Melted cold snow well at 130°F while pulling ~2.8A of 
current.  After successful ground tests, flew on VXE500.  We encountered light 
snow in flight without issue; also pulled ~2.8A in flight. Great job Laszlo!  
 E140ZG2 gimbals did not show correct location on map. Coordinates of gimbal 
position and slant range appeared to be way off. Neither of these gimbals were 
flown in Finland. Did not check E95 from Finland yet. Talked with Guks tonight: 
this is likely a map display issue due to a lack of DTED in the Epsilon software – 
we have a plan to resolve and re-test tomorrow.   
 CAPEX Planning - future demo/exercise deployment kits must include: 
o 1 50-200ft Ethernet cable (need to test with PoE on SGCS2) 
o Starlink system and cables in a transit case  
o 150-200ft extension cable 
o Power strip  
o Portable HD projector (bright) 
o Tie down strap kit (primarily for tie down of comms tripods and misc 
equipment in winds) 
o A variety of zip ties small to large and long 
o 20-50ft mast for SGCS2, ideally for SGCS3 too.  Can the Blue-sky mast 
work with SGCS3?

REMARKS 
 Magnetometer issues with fixed wing autopilot, required two calibrations.   
 Right as we were launching, the airspace coordinator informed us of 
potential jamming activities in the area.  We did not see any issues in 
flight with GPS or comms.  
 HLB cooled from 88°F at launch to 65°F in flight (approx. one hour) – 
something to keep an eye on for future flights. 
 Aircraft wings and prop blades coated in snow and frost on landing, 
pulled 460W in cruise – did we see effects of snow and frost? 
 After landing, we noted that the aircraft altitude said -70ft 
 Wing bolts and lift boom bolts required pliers to loosen. Parts were warm 
when assembled, then shrunk in the cold (like me)
