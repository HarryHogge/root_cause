# Land Abort Inop Reporting Procedure Form.docx

Attachment 63361, docx, 109 KB, added 2026-07-09 by Stephan Johnson.

This form is to be used to report mishaps and/or issues experienced during Redwire customer training and demonstration events. This form is for internal reporting purposes only.

Immediate Action Checklist

For a mishap involving an injury requiring medical attention, contact emergency services via cell phone (911). If operating on a military installation, adhere to local SOPs or preaccident plan for emergency services. Cooperate with emergency services as needed to secure the injured person(s)

Identify the mishap site (Record GPS coordinates from last known AV location, distance and bearing from GCS)

Preserve the UAS wreckage and mishap site, as appropriate/if possible

Take pictures of the aircraft and mishap site  o GCS screen shots, videos of launch and landing.

Secure and quarantine equipment that was involved in the mishap  o This includes the entire system such as GCS, radios, aircraft, payloads, and batteries

Gather and record names and contact information of all involved

Gather and record all system data (flight data recording, AAR files, mission files) o Refer to Post Incident Flight Data Collection

Note and record aircraft configuration and tail number (ie battery only, fuel cell, payload, etc)

Note and record all environmental conditions before and during the mishap (metar, current weather conditions, operational conditions)

Note and record all damage to aircraft

## Site Lead Check List for Mishaps

Verify Emergency services are responding as needed for injury, fire, or other hazardous situation requiring immediate attention

Notify everyone on the contact list as soon as possible.

Keep in contact with the Flight Operations Team

Verify mishap site is located and secured

Record names, contact info, and statements from all involved recording exactly what each did and/or saw about what happened to the UAS.

Verify wreckage is not removed or altered until released and/or all required data and information has been obtained o Equipment can be released depending on severity rating

As soon as reasonably possible consolidate and email all flight data, information gathered, pictures, and statements collected to UAS.support.rdt@rdw.com o Tag email as “High Importance” and include “Mishap Notification” in the subject line of the email

Global Support team will initiate root cause investigation through Jira utilizing data supplied by the Flight Operations Team

Global Support team will complete all required notifications for company leadership
