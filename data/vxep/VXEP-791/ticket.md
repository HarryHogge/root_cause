“AV flew fine for the entire flight, during recovery at about 40-60 ft the AV turned away from the wind and flew about 100 meters away while descending. It rapidly turned back and looked like it was going to finish landing at its new location, but drifted sideways before flipping over and dragging across our runway. Damages were as follows: broken tail boom, scrapped center wing, 2 broken prop blades, and a few of the center wing screws broke and were pushed into the center wing” Operator statement
