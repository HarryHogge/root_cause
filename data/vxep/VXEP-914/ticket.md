## Description

Current cases ship with desiccant bags. We do not offer replacement, and have no history of them wearing out (e.g. changing color due to max absorption). That said, it is unclear to users if it should be tossed, replaced at some point, etc.
Idea: Replace current desiccant bag with a tin/can desiccant, which is somehow installed in the lid of the case (to be out of the way). Ensure the status window is visible, and we can provide replacement in the TCI in case one ever gets fully used.
