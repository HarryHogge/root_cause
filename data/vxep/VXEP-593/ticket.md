## Comments (7)

### Noah Chavez, 2024-09-04

@Cameron Chapman 
All 133 Wing Shift Brackets in inventory were inspected by inserting an aluminum screw into each of the four holes. QTY 11 were found to have at least one improperly threaded hole, while the rest showed no defects. NCR 4211 was generated to track these defective parts and determine a repair disposition. A disposition has now been given to rework the affected brackets. We are working with procurement to notify the vendor and ensure their quality team is aware of this issue for future deliveries. Our receiving inspectors have also been instructed to check for this problem going forward.

### Cameron Chapman, 2024-09-04

### Jack Waeschle, 2024-09-04

@Cameron Chapman given their prevalence in the engineering bird inventory, I would expect non conforming brackets to be in the hands of customers currently. The risk of flying with a poorly secured wing is a flight sciences question, however it is not recommended. The hope is that an operator would recognize that the wing was not fully secured, and not proceed with the flight in that faulty configuration. Containment actions are a decision for @Greg Fryer
