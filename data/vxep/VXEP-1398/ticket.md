## Description

Hannah- all launch wizard steps were nominal prior to launch. Once launched, the battery balance window turned red in the VTOL Control Window. The aircraft paused its launch and began to descend. Pause was not available. Vehicle landed more harshly than nominal and compressed the landing pad. AAR retrieved as well as SA camera and screenshot post landing. Was unable to screengrab while vehicle was aloft.
Gus - I was performing as Ground Crew (technician) for this flight iteration. We were using cell phone to communicate to the PIC. All checks were normal and completed to standard. Upon launch the aircraft took off normal. Upon reaching approximately 150 feet the aircraft stopped ascent and the vertical motors appeared to be struggling to continue its climb and transition. I attempted to relay to the PIC to pause the aircraft to land less abruptly to no avail due to the aircraft coming down the way it did. The aircraft then came in contact with the ground and I requested the PIC "Cut motors and Safe the vehicle". Once motors were safe and cut I approached the vehicle to inspect for any damage. Upon initial inspection there was no damage besides the landing pad being more "squished in" due to the faster descent.
