## Description

Assemble, Retrofit, and Test QTY 1 Prototype MAGNSS aircraft for MARSOC
Prerequisites:
- Inventory of current BOM parts to build qty 1 PROTOTYPE MAGNSS retrofit shipset (Fuselage harness, Avionics Enclosure V3, FRPA Mk2)
- Qty 1 MARSOC VXE30 baseline aircraft for retrofit (GFX)

Scope:
Receive and retrofit qty 1 VXE30 aircraft to PROTOTYPE MAGNSS configuration (Avionics Enclosure V3 and FRPA Mk2). The MARSOC Prototype MAGNSS Retrofit, aka MADNSS, will conform to the same configuration as the previously delivered prototype configurations for HW, interfaces, and S/W. A S/W upgrade to the current MAGNSS 6.3.4 version will be delivered concurrently for all delivered prototypes.
Engineering will review current inventory posture of required BOM parts to assemble qty 1 Avionics Enclosure V3 (cables, hardware, PWAs); engineering will submit Production Requests for the build and/or modification of required components. Once all subassemblies are fabricated, engineering (EE and ME) will build and assemble the MAGNSS subsystem. The MAGNSS subsystem components will be retrofitted into the GFX aircraft following Expanded Payload Bay Opening (EPBO) modification in production. Software/firmware will be loaded in preparation for SCO and testing.
The MADNSS aircraft will undergo SCO, ground and flight testing. Testing is currently planned (but may change) to be performed without a payload.

GR&A
- If EA provides our only assembled developmental RGM for this effort, the RGM Productization effort will experience technical, cost, and schedule risk
- EA to receive qty 1 VXE30 baseline aircraft from MARSOC for this effort
- HD55 will not be able to be flown on the prototype
- Weight analysis of E140 and HD40 payloads will be performed to assess feasibility of flying MADNSS prototype with gimbal, but is not required

## Project Deliverables

Qty 1 MAGNSS Retrofit shipset installed on MARSOC GFX VXE30 aircraft w/6.3.4 MAGNSS S/W installed
Qty 1 Operational Supplement
