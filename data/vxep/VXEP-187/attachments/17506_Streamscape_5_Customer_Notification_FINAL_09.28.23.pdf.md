StreamScape 5 Release Announcement and Backwards Compatibility Alert  
 
Dear Customer: 
 
We are writing to inform you of the release of StreamScape 5, Silvus’ newest cutting-edge firmware, and 
with an important note about backwards compatibility.   
 
StreamScape 5  implements significant new LPI/LPD and AJ features designed to achieve Spectrum 
Dominance in contested environments including the following BETA enhancements: 
 
1. MANET Protected Waveform (MAN -PW) – when enabled, the MN -MIMO waveform is 
hardened for increased resiliency. For additional CUI information, contact your Silvus Sales 
Representative 
2. MANET Power Control (MAN-PC) – an LPD feature, MAN-PC minimizes the RF footprint 
of Silvus StreamCaster radios – dynamically throttling power to the minimum amount 
necessary to maintain network connectivity 
3. MANET Interference Avoidance & Cancellation (MAN -IAC) – Advanced interference 
avoidance and cancellation features working harmoniously to autonomously evade and filter 
jamming and interference 
 
StreamScape 5 also includes other important performance, security, a nd scalability improvements 
including improved transmit Eigen beamforming, FIPS 140-3 Level 2 encryption, and enhanced routing 
algorithms for  large-scale networks .  StreamScape 5 will also be fully compatible with the much -
anticipated StreamCaster SC5000 series radios.   
 
StreamScape 5 is operable on all pre -existing StreamCaster SC4000-series radios, and is immediately 
available for download and installation free of charge.  Customers with existing inventory of Silvus radios 
are encouraged to update their entire StreamCaster fleet to ensure full interoperability.   
 
Unfortunately, to accommodate important improvements and updates, 1  StreamScape 5 will not be 
compatible with prior firmware versions , and radios that are shipped from Silvus with SS5 installed 
may not be downgradable to SS4 .  Radios using older firmware will continue to operate as normal, but 
will be unable to communicate with newer radios until they are upgraded.   If you have compatibi lity 
concerns and would like to collaborate with Silvus on a custom transition plan, please contact our sales 
team at info@silvustechnologies.com. 
 
StreamScape 5 will begin shipping on all orders beginning November 1st. Customers looking to upgrade 
their existing radios should contact their sales representative for download and installation instructions. 
 
1 One critical update allows us to expand the number of unique node IDs which are each assigned to a particular radio.  The 
prior firmware was limited to a fixed maximum that is near depletion.  Changing this limitation has become necessary as a 
result of our ongoing success and continued sales.
