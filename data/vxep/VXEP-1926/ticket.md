## Description

Epsilon gimbal (E140Z G2) is working when connected to the aircraft of the ground, we are getting video feed and all is good.  Once the aircraft is launch the video screen turns to various colors of the rainbow.  We have tried power cycling it in the air via the aux payload switch in the custom payload page but it remains the same.  When on the ground and we power cycle the aircraft the video comes back but is lost again on launch.  We have used two aircraft 823 and 1016 and have had the same issue with both.
See picture attached and I have provide an AAR from 1016.
