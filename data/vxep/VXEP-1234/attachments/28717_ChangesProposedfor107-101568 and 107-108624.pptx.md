## Slide 2

Proposed changes
[picture]
Observing the data at left reveals that the board dimensions are bigger than the mount dimensions. It is obvious why the board does not fit into the mount. This proposal is to change the dimensions of the mount, 107-101568 as shown on page 4. The dimensions of the board need to be updated per page 3. Current fabrication of the board does not need to change.

## Slide 5

Proposed changes
The dimensional changes on the mount, 107-101658, would decrease the width shown below. This is the wall thickness that holds the board. The proposed dimensions would result in a wall thickness minimum of .060 and .068 based on the shown data. These minimums  are a result of minimum width dimensions, 3 and 4, minus maximum cavity dimensions, 1 and 2.
