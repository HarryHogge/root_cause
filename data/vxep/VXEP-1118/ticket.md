## Plan

Perform some preliminary testing with test flights to verify if we are confident in the structure of the pre-preg wing. This is NOT meant to be any form of robust qualification style testing at this point. Just a “gut-check” that the concept seems promising and we are not finding any glaring issues or suspect this new wing will cause a catastrophic failure during flight. 

Current Idea:
Pre-Flight - Assessment from SCO and FLOPS if wings perform any different (i.e. hinges cause different behavior with servo or something else)
Flight 1 - Normal Asset Flight to verify flight conditions and collect “control” data
Flight 2 - 1 Prepreg wing and 1 normal asset wet layup wing. Same flight pattern to collect flight data to directly compare against Flight 1 to see if the wing is somehow performing any different
(Potentially) Flight 3 - Both Prepreg wings to compare flight data against Flight 1 and Flight 2 to see if the wings are performing equivalent to normal wings
Post-Flight - Assessment from FLOPS if after flying the prepreg wings appear any different (i.e. any damage, delam, etc.)
