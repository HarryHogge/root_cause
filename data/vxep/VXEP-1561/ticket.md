## Description

Charger summary:
 Charger was being run off good generator power (constant and clean compared to most generators).
 Charger was charging HLB battery.
 Generator started to die and ended up going out. 
 Charger showed “fault power off 30 seconds”
 Crew plugged power extension chord into eco flow power bank (battery power supply). 
 Charger still shows fault code. 
 Soldier unplugged red power wire between charger and power supply. 
 Charger still shows fault as HLB was still plugged into charger. 
 Hlb was unplugged from charger.
 Charger turned off.
 Soldier waited 30 seconds and plugged red wire back into power supply from charger.
 Smell of fried electrical and sound of pop corn
 Unplugged immediately and gave to FSR onsite
