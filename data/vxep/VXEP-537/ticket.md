## Description

Evidence that short happened from main batt power lead (Fuselage Power Harness (p/n: 107-102189-2)) through fuselage. The negative power wire does not show exposed conductor, so the return path may be through other electronics. Vehicle needs evaluation. May not be a simple harness swap. 
Short happened when operator tried to demate power harness and contacted the sharp and conductive carbon. May have been preventable with pull straps, better connectors and contacts, etc.

## Plan

- replace fuse harness (p/n 107-102189-2)
- perform functional checkout with all fuse electronics
- radio
- AV box
- Nose cone
- replace avionics box
- once 300 has passed checkout pass to H. Jeanor to conduct testing for another critical MAGNSS effort (XKIT-2766)
- Investigate further effects of incident

## Issue Status

Vehicle has been evaluated and functionally tested. All electronics are operating as designed. Damaged harness replaced.
Waiting for AV Box replacement (planned before incident) and then will be passed off for MAGNSS testing
