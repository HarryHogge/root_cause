## Description

This is SSG Jackson Bahin with 3/75 UMS. We have had another incident with our second VXE30. We were flying it in Fuel Cell configuration and had a failure on launch. 
We were out at McKenna MOUT site on Fort Benning, Ga testing our fuel cell. I weighed and CG tested the vehicle. The weight was at 40 pounds and CG was between 4.7and 4.8. We conducted preflight through the launch wizard and everything was functional and within tolerance. All of the motor functions, battery levels, and power draws were within tolerance per the manual. The fuel cell was generating power and had prompted the HLB was sufficient for take-off.  
Upon reaching the 20ft altitude level all system functions appeared normal. once the aircraft gained elevation from 20ft agl i began to notice a cell imbalance in the VTOL control window and the lift motors gave an audible struggle. I also could see visually that the lift motors were not functioning properly. 
At this time i command an ABORT Launch and the vehicle responded with an RTL command in the VTOL control window. The vehicle struggled through descent and drifted about 50 ft back into the transportation boxes. The left lift boom snapped in half and several lift motor propellors broke but these were the only observed damages at this time.
