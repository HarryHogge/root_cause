## Description

Frank K
Operator states that after landing in fuel cell configuration they conducted a hot swap to relaunch the aircraft. During the hot swap they noticed smoke leaving the Aircraft from where the fuel cell is located. After shutting down all power and swapping VXE458 back into a battery configuration the operator states that center wing is no longer being detected by the aircraft. Operator said they have tried multiple power cycles and battery swaps and cannot connect. 

They have an additional fuselage enroute to their location and are going to see if the center wing can be detected on a different fuselage.

*Issue was reported Tuesday Sept 24th (hopefully this helps with sorting through data)
