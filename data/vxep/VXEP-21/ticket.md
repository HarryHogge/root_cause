## Description

loose article identified in the centre wing of 511. It could be heard rattling around. but couldn't be removed so Center wing Replaced it with 516. During the preflight inspection, the operator noted the sound of an object tumbling through the center wing when it is tilted end-over-end. Since this is not part of the wing design we chose to not fly the wing. We did not tear open the AP access panel to look for the object.
