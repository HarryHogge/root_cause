## Description

Launched 674 at 0830, after climbing about 50 to 75ft, the aircraft self-aborted the launch and VTOL prop speed fluctuations could be heard as the aircraft descended rapidly straight down back to the ground. The Aircraft landed just to the right side of the stand and the left rear VTOL blade broke on impact. No major damage was observed other than the broken VTOL blade. There is a slight flex mark on the underside of the center wing near the ADP. There is slight damage to the rear left VTOL motor cover. Upon removal of cover and inspection of motor, no damage was observed or felt.
HLB had 28 cycles. This is the 3rd occurrence of what we suspect is a cell split in the HLB during vtol launch within roughly 1 month.
