### Cameron Chapman, 2026-07-22

email chain Hey All,
There is not, and I don't necessarily think there should be a field repair for battery products. They are always carrying a dangerous amount of energy in them and should only be worked on by trained technicians in a setting that is equipped with proper safeguards.
As for repairs, these batteries are sealed shut and would need to be cut free from the case to replace this contact. Typically, that scope of rework is not considered worth the time and effort, and we replace the battery altogether. I would suggest treating it as an RMA and replacing it through that channel. 
I will let @Stephen Peters weigh in on whether this arc damage poses a significant risk, and how/whether it should be systematically tracked.
