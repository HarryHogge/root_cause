## Description

We have been seeing issues with the LEMB chargers incorrectly reporting the SOC of the batteries. 
Here is LEMB 0042 on the charger (not charging) at 77% and then put into the AC it is 69%.

We have also noticed that the charger reports a signifiactly higher SOC when the battery is charging. This has been misleading to students as they stop the charge and then it drops the SOC by 10-20% before unplugging and they have much less charge than expected.
