## Description

Reference Obsolescence Notification Jira https://jenaero.atlassian.net/browse/ENG-2820
“The Elmer’s Glue PN 100-102931 we use in Wet Layups is discontinued by the manufacturer. Sticky Ass Glue (PN 100-114007) was qualified as an alternate but is problematic (viscosity issues, drying/curing issues) and the team stopped using it a while back (we have 100 bottles in Inventory). “
