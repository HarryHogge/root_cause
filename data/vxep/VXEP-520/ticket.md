## Description

Develop VXE30 retract mechanisms for the Octopus E95 and E140 payloads.  
Requirements: 
- Optimize improved field of regard vs drag (less aft landing pad and airplane parts in the picture while we’re flying)
- Must be reliable and automatically retract upon landing (Use existing retract trigger during landing functionality)
- Minimize weight impacts to vehicle (GF: Must not exceed MGTOW with E140 gimbal)
- Must be compatible with VXE30 and Havoc payload bays (Existing Avionics and comms enclosure, MAGNSS and RGM, HLBs, and tanks)
- The top payload plate must include an Octopus GPS antenna and support the antenna cable to the payload
- GF Q: Do we have a location-precision requirement? Existing mounts are very loose, should it fasten down?
- Payload assemblies must be installed into the vehicle without tools, allowing for quick battery and fuel tank swaps
- Some customers will continue to use Trillium, some will use Octopus.  So, we must maintain interchangeability/compatibility with the existing Trillium retract electrical/software command interface. Use the retract PWM signal on the gray Alden, match Trillium electrical and PWM configuration if possible.
- Must be capable of operation in rain, snow, dust, sand from -20C to 50C (-4F to 122F)
- Design shall not cause EMI/EMC issues with the payload or avionics (in either deployed or retracted position, and while operating)
- Consideration should be given to manufacturability and manufacturing cost
- No solenoids (Scott S can buy Sam M a drink for including this requirement)

Goals: 
- Deploy beyond the aft landing pad, matching or exceeding Trillium’s deployment.  Consider pectoral fins instead of aft pad (separate, but related projecthttps://jenaero.atlassian.net/browse/VXEP-521 ).
- Install onto existing Octopus payloads and aircraft without modification of the design/drilling holes
- Ability to deploy and retract
- Same retract for E95 and E140 (not sure if this is practical or possible)
