## Description

User states that they had the main chargers installed into a 20 AMP circuit and were pulling 6 amps total and stepped away from the charger several minutes went by and then heard an explosion originating from the area where the chargers were connected. 

SN were burned off most of the batteries but here is a list of the batteries on site
6ea x VXE30 Main (FCR) Battery (P/N: 107-101050 | S/Ns: 3-2424, 3-2419, 3-2422, 3-2394, 3-2319, 3-2411)

Purpose of the ticket is to find out if there have been any reports of MAINS catching fire, if the charger/power supply they were making was out of date.
please let me know if you require any additional data we will find anything that is available.

## Plan

- Two ideas/questions:
- Do we log cell voltages during charging on the BMS? If not, could we in the future?
- Two main battery chargers on one power supply will exceed the power rating of the blue ones (I don't remember the black one rating). Do we know how reliable the overcurrent/power protection is on them? Do we know if they were using one power supply per charger, or possibly 2 chargers on one supply?
