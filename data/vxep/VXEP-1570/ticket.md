## Description

RPAS failure to descend at landing site. On the last sortie today, EAGLE returned for standard vertical landing above the LZ however once it had transitioned to the hover (approx 300 ft AGL) a CLIMB ABORT - LIFT SYSTEM FAULT caption was displayed and the UA did not begin its descent. After approx 5 mins of fault diagnosis, the RP took manual control and by manoeuvring laterally appeared to be able to ‘wash-off’ some of the height and the UA made a very slow descent to a safe landing (landing aprox 15 mins after initially entering the hover). Initial thoughts are that a combination of light aircraft (with high aspect ratio), coupled with moderate on-shore winds and cliff-top landing site generated vertical air currents that resulted in excess lift and an the inability to descend (this is backed up with low power-draw on the lift battery and the vertical lift motors being seen to start/stop whilst in the hover, suggesting it required little to no power to remain in the hover).  Mission data has been sent to LM (via UAS DT) for advice.
