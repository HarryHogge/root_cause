## Description

Taking a look at the drawing for the Boom Harness (107-103181), the dimensions are not very clear. The dimensions for the Boom Power Harness (107-103094) to the heat shrink are not specified.  In the case of the Boom Harness on NCR-230824 - 1418, from the breakout to the tip of the ODU, the determined length was insufficient at 2 inches. However, upon inspecting Boom Harnesses that are in inventory, we discovered those to be around 2.25 inches which have not caused any issues. 
Problem:
NCR due to Final Assy being unable to install the Boom Harness due to the wire length from the breakout to the ODU being too short to fit into the plate in the lift boom.
Investigation:
We found that the harness drawing does not call out this length at all even though it is critical to being able to install this cable. The procedure includes info on aligning the JST side.
Request:
Engineering Drawing should include a clear requirement for required lengths to be able to properly install the cable. In this case ~2.25” from the breakout to the front of the ODU is needed in Final Assy. Need to evaluate how to call that out and inspect to it at a lower level.
