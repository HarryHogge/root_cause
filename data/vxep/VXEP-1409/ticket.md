## Description

Implement EMI shielding for the VBN enclosure and fuselage on the 35Y prototype using approved materials (e.g., 3M 1170/1181 tape or triple-stack tape). Ensure proper electrical coupling, coordinate timing with production, and document all work for traceability and future builds.
Deliverables:
- Completed EMI shielding installation on the VBN enclosure and mating surfaces.
- Visual documentation (photos) and a short install report for the project records.
- Confirmation of electrical continuity (where practical) between shielding and airframe.

## Plan

Action Items:
Receive Components:
Confirm receipt of painted VBN enclosure and trimmed fuselage from production.Ensure all required shielding materials, tapes, and fasteners are on hand.Prepare for Application:
Inspect VBN enclosure and fuselage for any issues prior to shielding.Clean surfaces as needed for optimal tape adhesion.Apply Shielding:
Apply EMI shielding tape (3M 1170/1181 or equivalent) inside the VBN enclosure and to any required fuselage mating surfaces.Ensure tape makes proper electrical contact with conductive fasteners or designated grounding points.Verify Installation:
Perform a basic electrical continuity check between the applied shielding and the fuselage.Document results and take photos of the install for reference.Handoff & Recordkeeping:
Notify team that EMI shielding is complete.Upload install documentation to project files/SharePoint.Hand off for final integration and assembly as required.
