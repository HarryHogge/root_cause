2.1: Position the replacement avionics module in the fuselage with the serial number sticker facing the nose and within reach of the black aircraft wire harness connector. Using the alignment dot and arrow as a guide, push the black aircraft wiring harness connector into the receptacle on the avionics module. The latching feature should lock into place with an audible “click”. Gently pull in line with the axis of the connector to verify the latching feature is engaged.
2.2: Apply a small bead of silicone sealant (100-102206) to the junction where the aircraft wire harness connector meets the receptacle on the avionics module. The objective is to provide visual confirmation that the connection is latched and to stake the barrel of the aircraft wire harness connector, so the latching feature stays engaged and doesn’t inadvertently disconnect during flight.
