## Comments (3)

### Jack Waeschle, 2024-09-04

I measured the pure bending and torsional stiffness of this wing and compared it with standard VXE30 wing data, as summarized below.
[embedded: image-20240904-230003.png]
In messier, visual form with a bunch of XKit wing stiffness data cluttering up this comparison: 
[embedded: image-20240904-230108.png]
In short, with delaminations, this extensively old wing is slightly more flexible in pure bending but stiffer in torsion. This should not affect normal flight performance. However, I would not fly triclops on this wing. Given the rest of 300 is already a hodgepodge of cut holes and gaffers tape, a few delaminations in the wing should not be a concern. If 300 gets a makeover/new fuselage and we want it to be a suitable show pony bird again, it should have a new wing.
One wing bolt was chopped/broken off short, and this should be replaced.

### Jack Waeschle, 2024-09-04

There is no viable repair for delaminations of this sort. The only real repair is a complete center wing replacement, but I think after a bolt replacement, this can be used as is for our engineering purposes.

### Jack Waeschle, 2024-09-10

Replaced wing bolt. Wing suitable for engineering flight
