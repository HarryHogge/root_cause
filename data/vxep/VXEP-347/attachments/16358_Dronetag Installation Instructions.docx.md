## Scope

## Application

This document applies to VXE30 aircraft where remote identification is required for operation and the user has selected and purchased a Dronetag Beacon with Edge Autonomy supplied mounting kit.

## Background

United States Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Title 14 Part 89.105 Remote Identification Requirement, outlines the necessity of Remote Identification for unmanned aircraft. Other countries may have alternative requirements and users should refer to current regulations of the applicable airspace to be operated within.

Edge Autonomy selected the Dronetag Beacon as a means to become compliant with remote identification requirements. This selection does not necessarily mean an endorsement of the product or manufacturer. VXE30 users may select alternative solutions to become compliant. However, other solutions will be incompatible with the mounting hardware provided by Edge Autonomy for the Dronetag Beacon.

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to provide the installation and basic operating instructions for the Dronetag Beacon and Edge Autonomy mounting kit.

## Parts Required

1x Dronetag Beacon

1x Edge Autonomy Dronetag Mounting Kit consisting of

PN 107-112978 Dronetag Beacon Enclosure

-01 Dronetag Enclosure Body

-02 Dronetag Enclosure Lid

PN 107-112791 Dronetag Harness

## Tools and Supplies

1x #1 Phillips Screwdriver

## Installation

Step 1. Remove the aft 2 screws from the AUX Payload connector housing using the #1 Phillips screwdriver.

Step 2. Install Dronetag in enclosure body with the Dronetag USB port facing the enclosure body cutouts. Place enclosure lid over body to secure Dronetag within body.

Step 3. Install the mount with tabs facing forward using removed screws and #1 Phillips screwdriver.

(Optional) Step 4. For charging or for operation greater than 6-8hrs, use the power harness provided. Connect the power harness to the AUX Payload connector.

Power to the hardware may be configured on or off by selecting the respective function within the Custom Payload Control dialog in the Ground Control Station (GCS) software for Aux2 Power.

## Operation

Refer to Dronetag’s website (help.dronetag.cz/dronetag-beacon) for the latest information regarding operation.

Long press (2 s): Switch the device on or off

Short press (0.1 s): Start flight or end flight (while the device is turned on)

Short press (0.1 s): Battery indication (while the device is turned off)

Extra-long press (4-8 s): Force device hardware restart
