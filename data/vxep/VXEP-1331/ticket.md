## Description

Design and prove out a “plan B” where the off-the-shelf dev-kit (with USB) is housed in a simple retrofit enclosure that drops into the B40 fuselage in case Sierra’s rigid-flex PCB is delayed or fails testing.


Deliverables
- 3D CAD for retrofit enclosure (STP + drawing)
- Mounting/fastener list and BOM
- Wiring diagram showing USB feed-through and power/tie-ins
- Fit-check report or photos in a B40 airframe
- Go/No-Go recommendation once PCB testing completes
