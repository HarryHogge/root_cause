This form is to be used to report mishaps and/or issues experienced during Edge 
Autonomy training, exercises, and demonstration events. 
*FOR INTERNAL REPORTING PURPOSES ONLY*
Immediate Action Checklist 
⎯ For a mishap involving an injury requiring medical attention contact emergency services 
via cell phone (911).  
⎯ If opera ting on a military installation, adhere to local SOPs or pre -accident plan for 
emergency services.  
⎯ Cooperate with emergency services as needed to secure the injured person(s). 
⎯ Identify the mishap site (Record GPS coordinates  from last known AV location , distance 
and bearing from GCS). 
⎯ Preserve the UAS wreckage and mishap site, as appropriate/if possible. 
⎯ Take pictures of the aircraft and mishap site. 
▪ GCS screen shots, videos of launch and landing
⎯ Secure and quarantine equipment that was involved in the mishap. 
▪ This includes  the entire system such as GCS, radios, aircraft, payloads, and
batteries.
⎯ Gather and record names and contact information of all involved. 
⎯ Gather and record all system data (flight data recording, AAR files, mission files). 
▪ Refer to Post Incident Flight Data Collection
⎯ Note and record aircraft configuration and tail number (i.e. battery only, fuel cell, payload, 
etc.). 
⎯ Note and record all environmental conditions before and during the mishap  (METAR, 
current weather conditions, operational conditions). 
⎯ Note and record all damage to aircraft.

Site Lead Checklist for Mishaps/Issues 
⎯ Verify Emergency Services are responding as needed for injury, fire, or other hazardous 
situation requiring immediate attention. 
⎯ Notify everyone on the contact list as soon as reasonably possible. 
⎯ Keep in contact with the Flight Operations Team. 
⎯ Verify mishap site is located and secured. 
⎯ Record names, contact info, and statements from all involved recording exactly what each 
did and/or saw about what happened to the UAS.    
⎯ Verify wreckage is not removed or altered until released and/or all required data, and 
information has been obtained. 
▪ Equipment can be released depending on severity rating.
⎯ As soon as reasonably possible consolidate and email all flight data, information gathered, 
pictures, and statements collected to GlobalSupport@edgeautonomy.io. 
▪ Tag email as “High Importance” and include “Mishap Notification” in the subject
line of the email.
⎯ Global Support team will initiate root cause investigation through Jira utiliz ing data 
supplied by the Flight Operations Team. 
⎯ Global Support team will complete all required notifications for company leadership.
