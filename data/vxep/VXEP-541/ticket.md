## Description

Information reported:
“SOCOM PM discovered that the pressure release valve for the vent line while filling aircraft propane tank does not have a max pressure rating. Hose detached from valve while filling the propane tank (5/13) and propane was spewing from valve end. I believe this situation occurred more likely due to Technician not properly securing the fuel line.

## Plan

Add label to valve
Update to training and manuals on hose connection requirements during filling
