## Description

During the second flight of the CBP B40 training VXE 1008 seemed to have excessive wobble during landing. Operators said it was significantly different from the first landing and was audibly and visibly odd compared to the first landing. VXE 1008 has been grounded until a data review and greenlight from engineering. Please see the attached data and video.
