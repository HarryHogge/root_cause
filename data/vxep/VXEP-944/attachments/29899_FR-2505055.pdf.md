Introduction
Analysis Procedure/Results
Hi-Rel Laboratories received three ethernet switches for analysis from Edge Autonomy LLC. The 
ethernet switches were identified by the part number KSZ8895RQX and manufactured by Microchip. It 
was reported that one of the switches was internally shorted. It was requested to determine the cause 
of failure. The switches were serialized as follows:
• U11: device on a PCBA, reported as being internally shorted
• U12: device on a PCBA, reported as being suspect
• New: a known good device sent for comparison
• As-received Inspection
• The as-received inspection revealed evidence of rework and damaged leads for the ‘U11’ device. 
This was done prior to Hi-Rel’s receipt to isolate the failure.
• No other anomalies were observed.
• Radiographic Inspection
• Radiographic inspection was performed using a Fein Focus 160 Cougar Radiographic Inspection 
System.
• Inspection revealed no anomalies.
• Electrical Testing
• Electrical testing was performed using a Fluke 117 True RMS Multimeter.
• Testing revealed degradation/short-circuits of the VDDIO, VDDAT, VDDAR, and MDIXDIS 
pins for both of the ‘U11’ and ‘U12’ devices.
Hi-Rel Laboratories, Inc Report FR-2505055 2
Analysis Procedures/Results

## Page 3

Analysis Procedure/Results - Continued
• Scanning Acoustic Microscopy (SAM)
• SAM was performed to inspect for delamination and evidence of damage to the packaging of the 
devices.
• No significant delamination or damage to the packaging was observed.
• Removal from the PCBA
• The ‘U11’ and ‘U12’ devices were removed from the PCBA using a hot air reflow gun.
• Optical / Radiographic inspection of the devices after removal from the PCBA revealed no 
anomalies.
• Retesting of the ‘U11’ device revealed degradation/short-circuits of the VDDAT, VDDAR, and 
MDIXDIS pins. The initially measured short-circuit for the VDDIO pin was cleared.
• No degradation/short-circuit conditions were observed for the ‘U12’ device. The ‘U12’ 
device measured resistance values similar to the ‘New’ device, indicating the device was 
not failed.
• Decapsulation
• Decapsulation was initially performed using laser ablation to remove the majority of the 
encapsulant covering the die. The remaining encapsulant was chemically removed using hot 
fuming nitric acid.
• Internal Visual Inspection
• Inspection of the ‘U11’ die revealed damage to several bond pads located at one of the corners of 
the die. The pins correlating to the damaged bond pads were MDIXDIS, GNDA,
IN_PWR_SEL, and LDO_O.
• No evidence of electrical overstress or anomalies were observed for the ‘U12’ die.
Hi-Rel Laboratories, Inc Report FR-2505055 3

## Page 4

Analysis Procedure/Results - Continued
• Infrared (IR) Hotspot Detection
• IR hotspot detection was performed on the ‘U11’ device to verify the observed damage sites and 
potentially locate additional damage sites.
• Supplying 7mA to the MDIXDIS pin revealed a hotspot at the damaged bond pad of the 
MDIXDIS pin.
• Supplying 7mA to the VDDAR pin revealed a hotspot at the MDIXDIS bond pad and at a 
metal line, likely related to the damage to VDDAR.
• Deprocessing
• The ‘U11’ device was deprocessed layer by layer until the silicon was reached to further 
document the damage sites.
• Optical / Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) upon reaching the silicon revealed damage sites 
in the MDIXDIS and IN_PWR_SEL pin’s I/O structures.
• The observed damage is consistent with damage from an overvoltage condition.
• No damage to the I/O structure of the LDO_O pin was noted, however damage was observed in 
the die metallization/oxide layers as shown here.
Hi-Rel Laboratories, Inc Report FR-2505055 4

## Page 5

Summary/Conclusion
The failure of the ‘U11’ device was verified. The ‘U11’ device failed due to an overvoltage condition on 
the 3.3V rail. This resulted in damage to the MDIXDIS, IN_PWR_SEL and LDO_O circuits and 
subsequent malfunction of the 3.3V and 1.2V supply.
The observed damage is not consistent with electrostatic discharge (ESD), and no evidence of any 
manufacturing defects was noted in this analysis.
