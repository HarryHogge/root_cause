Low profile MLDM connectors have reduced flange height compared to 
standard MWDM Micro-D connectors. These .050" pitch solder cup Micro-D 
connectors accept #26 to #30 gage wire with standard contacts and up to size 
#24 wire with “large bore” contacts. Contacts are factory-installed and potted 
with epoxy. Pin contacts are gold-plated high performance TwistPin type and 
are recessed into insulator to prevent damage. Socket contacts are gold plated, 
machined copper alloy. Machined aluminum shell. Glass-filled high temperature 
LCP thermoplastic insulators withstand soldering heat. Meets performance 
requirements of MIL-DTL-83513. Available with 9 to 51 contacts. 3 amps, 600 Vac, 
-55°C to +150°C.
