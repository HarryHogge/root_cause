## Description

Occasionally we have an issue where the Vertical Tail Retainer on the Vertical Tail clashes with the Stab Saddle on the Tail Boom Assy. This appears to just be a tolerance stackup issue and although it can cause a bit more resistance when installing the Vertical Tail to the Tail Boom very occasionally the interference is enough to cause difficulty installing and/or removing the vertical tail. This shows as mark-off between the parts as well.

Breif discussion with Scott and Greg and probably a small dimensional adjustment on either the saddle or the retainer should resolve this.

Tail Boom Assy: 107-102175
Stab Saddle: 107-102746

Vertical Tail Assy: 107-101205
Vertical Tail Retainer: 107-102074

As of 11/22/23 most recent noticeable interference was on AC 577

In agreement with Engineering the standing Disposition as of 12/15/23 when this is found is as follows:
Rework
Adjust 107-102746 saddle as far forward as it will go
if needed sand 107-102074 vertical tail retainer slightly to provide clearance
apply touch up paint to tail boom saddle
