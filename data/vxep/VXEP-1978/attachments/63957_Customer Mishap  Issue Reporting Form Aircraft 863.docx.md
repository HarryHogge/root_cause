| Statements of all personnel involved…      Aircraft: 863       GCS S/N: SGCS1-279     Antenna S/N:      Payload Type: 140ZG2 LWIR     Payload S/N: EPS6282     Main Battery: 3-2337     HLB: 2-4575         External Injuries, Damage, etc: None     Weather Conditions: Clear/90s/Less than 90MPH On 8/6/2026 at approximately 21:00, VXE-30 #863 suffered a catastrophic failure, resulting in a crash.   #863 was being operated by the Tucson Sector Border Patrol VXE Team, all members of which have been trained to operate the platform by Redwire.  The crash happened near San Miguel Arizona.   Approximately 1.5 hours into the flight, the primary GCS running the SUAV software reported a loss of link notification.  The situational camera on the primary GCS, as well as the payload camera on the secondary GCS never lost communication to their respective payloads and continued to provide a video feed. The operators believed that the aircraft would regain connection automatically, but that did not happen.  After a short time of loss link status, the aircraft began to veer towards the pre-programmed loss link waypoint, however that cannot be confirmed, as the loss like waypoint location was already in the general direction that the aircraft was traveling when the loss of link occurred.   After the primary GCS failed to reconnect to the aircraft, the operator attempted to manually reconnect.  That also was unsuccessful.  The operator then attempted to close the SUAV software and restart it.  The operator noted after the software was restarted and clicked connect, the pre-populated tail number was 999. A few seconds later the secondary operator noticed on the payload camera that the aircraft appeared to be rapidly descending towards the ground and appeared to cut out as the aircraft made impact.  The GPS coordinates from the payload camera were noted, and the operators proceeded to those GPS coordinates and located the aircraft at those exact coordinates. The aircraft appeared to have struck mesquite branches just before impact into the ground.  The aircraft and payload sustained extensive damage and appear to be a total loss.  The aircraft and payload were recovered and transported to the Tucson Sector Headquarters SUAS office and will be secured for further inspection/processing by Redwire. Please advise of the next steps you would like, including any diagnostics or shipment to a Redwire facility. |
