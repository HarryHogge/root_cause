Testing the ODU power contacts ( )TAC power contacts showed that, as expected, with crimped and low resistance contacts the heat from the constant current will build up in the wires. In this case, the 8 AWG wire reached 160C in about 2minutes30seconds which is well within our current lift profile. The contacts themselves reached about 120C in the same time. This moves the failure point from the connector to the solder joints on the Dual Current Sense Board or battery leads themselves.
For our current electrical purposes, these contacts would suffice. Closing task
