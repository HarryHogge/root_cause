## Description

PN 106-103720 calls for 2:1 heatshrink, pn 100-105438. 2:1 ADH is unnecessary since our standard ADH heatshrink is 3:1. This should be switched with 3:1 heatshrink, pn 100-106124, and the 2:1 should be removed from inventory. A previous ticket has been opened to go through and identify all the builds that include 2:1 ADH heatshrink so that we can help purchasing, inventory, and production avoid errors by eliminating non-standard material. PN 100-105438 should especially be removed from the vault so that it does not get used in future designs and BOM’s and ensure that only pn 100-106124 is available for use during the design of future parts.
