## Description

If production replaces the wet layup department with “the airfoil department” (Wingtips, horizontal and vertical tails), these new parts will need verification testing to assure customers these prepreg parts are functionally equivalent to the wet layup predecessors.
These tests include:
- Dynamic wobble susceptibility
- Repeat the MOD Payload antenna testing Ryan Christino and I completed 2.5 years ago
- Add wingtip antenna, drive at dash speeds on runway with center panel, boom and wingtip mounted to truck bed, add weights on aft end of mockup antenna until flutter is induced
- Static deflection testing
- Measure deflection with weights added to wing tip


Tests I would not like to include:
- Destructive test of wingtip
- The spar is unchanged, this is the only structural component of the wing.
- “Abuse Test”
- Hard to quantify wingtip susceptibility to normal scrapes and bumps
- This is being considered while developing the wingtip prepreg layup schedule, trying to maintain durability, but these will not pass the “bash the wingtip against the edge of a table” test that Elias pioneered.
- Our aircraft is now VTOL, crash resilience is not a functional requirement of the vehicle anymore. Testing specifically for this will be hard to sell to the customer in a technical report.
