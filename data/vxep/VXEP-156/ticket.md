## Description

From customer:
398 has not been flown since it was sent back from Edge late August ( we only used wingset). When ground testing this week there was a smell, and main battery removed. The battery cable was hot to touch and  O rings on the plugs were burnt. This happened twice this week, the first time it was chalked up to a bad battery. The second time it happened with a different battery, so we narrowed it down to aircraft side.
Questions we asked the customer and their response:
Perform resistance measurement from the tip of the AS150 anti-spark contact to the base. Report findings.
 3.5 base to base negative-pos , 0.9 inside negative to base of positive
Measure resistance between positive (from the base of the terminal) and negative terminals.
9.8 tip to base positive pin (this seems high??)  it was 5.7 on the other birds
Aircraft is enroute on RMA as of 10/25/23
