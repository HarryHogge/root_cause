## Description

Avery labels 5526 on the vehicle cases have been peeling after flight test. New label needed.

Investigation thus far:
Surfaces are prepped with alcohol and the sticker is squeegeed on. They peel after coming back from flight test. The labels are supposed to be UV resistant.
5526 label has switched to “ultrahold” adhesive. The instances of peeling we have found have all been the ultrahold backing. Reference NCR 1219

Theory:
1 - Transit cases are heating up and changing shape more out at Huasna then they were before since its summer and it gets hotter out there than SMR. As the case gets hot, expands, and moves around the adhesive is flexing to move with the case. When the case comes back to the shop and cools down and “shrinks” the stickers now look disbonded and deformed with bubbles because the plastic reset. 
2 - Manufacturer changed adhesive and it no longer bonds as well to the type of plastic of the tan transit cases. 

Potential Testing:
Testing for Theory 1: Transit Case is changing shape
Apply labels, mark the case with dots/lines around the labels, put in hotbox. 
- See if the dots/lines visible change which would show the case is changing
- See if we can detect how the label is impacted by that
- Maybe apply a label while case is hot…?
- Maybe apply labels in different positions/locations
- Maybe apply labels that are different shapes?

Pull case out, let it cool, 
- Did the dots/lines return to normal?
- Can we correlate the label disbonding to the way the case changed shape?
- Are there some areas of the case more or less suseptible to change in shape? i.e. are flat areas better than corners…? Maybe just a position change of the label would help.


Testing for Theory 2: Adhesive change
Apply some Labels using an adhesion promoter, put in hotbox
Let cool and compare adhesion between labels with and without promoter
