## This form is to be used to report mishaps and/or issues experienced during Edge Autonomy customer training and demonstration events. This form is for internal reporting purposes only.

## Immediate Action Checklist for mishaps

For mishap involving an injury requiring medical attention contact emergency services via cell phone (911). Cooperate with emergency services as needed to secure the injured person(s)

Identify the mishap site (Record GPS coordinates, etc.)

Contact the Flight Operations Manager

Preserve the UAS wreckage and mishap site, as appropriate/if possible

Secure equipment that was not damaged and account for all equipment

Gather and record names and contact information of all involved

Gather and record all system data (flight data recording, AAR files, etc)

Note and record aircraft configuration and tail number (ie battery only, fuel cell, payload, etc)

Note and record all environmental conditions before and during the mishap

Note and record all damage to aircraft

Take pictures of the aircraft and mishap site

## Flight Operations Manager Check List for mishaps

Ensure Emergency services are responding as needed for injury, fire, or other hazardous situation requiring immediate attention

Call everyone on the contact list and record who you talked to, date; time and the out come of the conversation.

Notify necessary personnel within three hours of initial notification of incident

Keep in contact with the Flight Operations Team

Verify mishap site is located and secured

Record names, contact info, and statements from all involved recording exactly what each did and/or saw about what happened to the UAS.

Ensure wreckage is not removed or altered until released and/or all required data and information has been obtained

Gather and preserve all flight data

Provide within three hours via email all flight data, information gathered, pictures, and statements collected to GlobalSupport@edgeautonomy.io

Global Support team will initiate root cause investigation through Jira utilized data supplied by the Flight Operations Team

Tag email as “High Importance” and include “Mishap Notification” in the subject line of the email

Global Support team will complete all required notifications to company leadership
