Sealing Air Data Pod Improvement Project
Schedule
Problem Statement
When building the Air Data Board, technicians have to pot the board in multiple layers of Toughseal.  This requirement is driven because the Air Data Pod is not considered a sealed cavity and therefore needs all subassemblies within to be waterproofed.
It takes an estimated 4 hours of touch time and 3.5 hours of cure time to process a batch of 12 ADPs. The SA Cam can possibly have the Kona removed from it if replaced with thermal gap pads, saving an estimated 1 hour of touch time per batch.
Proposed Solution
Sealing the Air Data Pod would likely be doable using RTV 732 and would remove the need for potting from the Air Data Board and SA cam.  It would also use significantly safer materials to help with current safety goals.
Measures of Success
Measures will be overall time spent processing the Air Data Pod subassemblies through the electronics shop and total time spent sealing the Air Data Pod in final assembly.
Goal is to reduce the number of adhesive applications down to only those required for maintaining thermal properties of the SA cam, RTV sealing of the static port of the Air Data Board, and sealing of the Air Data Pod.
Will save approximately 3 hours of touch time and 2.5 hours of cure time in processing Air Data Board batches alone.
