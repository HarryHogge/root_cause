## Description

After deliveries of 2122839-02, There was marked increase in failures when several boards were found dead on arrival; during initial testing, the barometers reported zero. This affected approximately thirty to forty percent of the units. Boards from two batches remained in inventory – one delivered in December and the other in April. To determine whether the issue was batch‑specific, Gary tested 20 boards from the April shipment. Nearly half of them failed (11 out of 20). This included another barometer failure and ten boards with accelerometers outside the specification limits defined in Kurt’s procedure.
For two cases, the barometer was replaced which did fix the board.  However, these were experimental attempts and reflowing or re-working the component in-house is not feasible since the barometer is sensitive to that kind of heat.  
Conclusion.
Barometer failures are likely limited to a batch issue from the December deliveries.  There are 28 boards of this lot left in inventory.
Accelerometer failures make up 9 out of 20 of the recently delivered lot of 125 boards from Green Circuits.  Only one of these failures also had a dead barometer.
A yield of 95% success rate is acceptable.  The decision is, thus, to accept such a yield and not pursue this any further for the moment.
