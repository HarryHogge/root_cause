12. EVENT SUMMARY (Provide a brief explanation of the incident.):
During operation, VXE30 Stalker experienced loss of video feed and loss of link at approximately 6823 FT MSL. MO commanded the AV to RTB and was able to reconnect AV and Video Feed at 5363 FT MSL. MO continued to RTB, during the AV’s decent, MO lost video feed at approximately 3800 FT MSL and lost link again at 3287 FT MSL. The AV has not been pinged on the Silvus Topology and has not been found.
13. ACTIONS TAKEN UPON/AFTER LOSS (Search pattern used, number of searchers, duration of search, etc).
 An initial search was conducted immediately via ground vehicle armed with a GCS laptop and short range silvus radio in efforts of pinging the Stalkers location. Multiple teams composed of the battalions scout teams and 29 Palms Tulsa team have been disbursed as of 0930 this morning in efforts to recovery the lost stalker and is currently in progress.
14. SUMMARY OF DAMAGE (Provide a description of the damage to the AV)
ESTIMATED THAT THE STALKER CRASHED INTO A MOUNTAIN, HOWEVER, SEARCH IS STILL ONGOING AND STALKER HAS NOT BEEN FOUND TO DETERMINE DAMAGES.
