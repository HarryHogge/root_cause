## Description

During a recent flight, the customer experienced issues with the FFC/NUC procedure stating that the IR image would freeze after conducting the procedure.
Also, after power cycling the gimbal, the GPS indicator tab would not turn green (Lock).

## Corrective Action

provided on site training and firmware updates to camera, awaiting October release of the MWIR firmware to alleviate dted issue

## Plan

Updating to the latest software and firmware should alleviate the IR image freezing.
The GPS tab can take up to 10 minutes to re-acquire sats and go from yellow to green.

## Executive Summary

Customer has flown with redwire personnel multiple times and we have gotten the issue down to just a dted bug in the MWIR firmware, other issues have been alleviated with on onsite training and proper gimbal cables
