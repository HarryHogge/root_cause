I've attached a product sheet for the ice detection sensor. We have a new version in our pipeline, which is smaller, lighter, and includes more features.
We use carbon fiber for heat elements. We generally provide the carbon parts, i.e., propellers wings, empennage. We build them so they are a copy of what you have already, they just come with the added benefit, that we have control of the surface temperatures at icing exposed areas.
- First, the sensor is required. One or more, depending on your requirements for redundancies.
- The pull propeller and VTOL propellers are critical to protect from ice as we experience that they ice up to unsustainable levels within minutes. We would provide propellers to your design, motors to your preference, power transmission units, and control units.
- Aerodynamic surfaces, e.g., wings and tail are critical, but in our experience, it takes 10's of minutes before they ice up to an unsustainable level. I would suggest this as a secondary or subsequent focus. If protection is needed for these surfaces as well, we would provide the entire carbon part to your design, with ice protection embedded.
