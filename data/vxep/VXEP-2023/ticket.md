## Description

The flights were conducted on Monday, 5 Oct 26.
 
V1/2 stated that the USMC Fleet unit was experiencing concerns/issues while operating. The System elevated to 8700’ AGL after commanding the AV (AV 853) to RTB and would not descend normally. The Operators worked the concern/issue for over an hour by permitting elongated racetracks to give the AV time to descend. The Operators were able to successfully recover the System. The Operators believe it was caused by a various of aspects which include, but not limited to, bad pitot static system, overheated avionics, temperature exceeding 115 Fahrenheit, updrafts and maybe additional unknown causes.
 
Currently, the VXE30 Stalker System is quarantined until the issue is identified. The USMC Fleet unit launched a different AV tail number and performed a RTB test to determine whether the issue persisted, and it did not. The new AV tail number executed the RTB flawlessly.
