	Kurt

	Thanks for the email.

	I believe that we decided that the length of the current bracket accommodates two 80 cm reflectors. The reflective area of the 80 cm is nearly 80% larger than the 60 cm and should result in a gain increase of as much as 2.0 dB. The 80cm carbon fiber reflector costs $4,739 each or $9,478 for both. The 80cm is segmented and two reflectors will fit in the case with the feeds. A longer bracket could hold two 1m reflectors which would increase the gain by more than 3.0 dB. The 1m costs $5,380, $10,760 for two.

	Let's discuss options.

	Jim
