CTM
AimLock’s Core Targeting Module (CTM) computing technology is used in intelligent precision targeting small arms systems that 
combine sensor management, computer vision, artificial intelligence, networks, and sensor fusion with advanced fire control, target 
tracking, and platform management to create speed and accuracy never achieved before with individual, remote, and unmanned 
weapon systems.
AimLock integrates purpose-built systems that include individual auto-targeting weapon stabilization chassis for semi-automatic 
rifles (I-M1), medium sized remote weapon stations with a medium machine gun payload suitable for mounting on ground vehicles, 
watercraft, helicopters, and fixed site installation (R-M1), and small and micro remote weapon stations suitable for mounting on 
various unmanned aerial systems and tripod installations (R-S1). 
Common to each system is our precision targeting package featuring integrated but abstracted day/night sensors, active 
stabilization, autonomous target detection, target tracking, target classification, and automatic ballistic solutions. This system 
enables the shooter to hit static and moving targets from either a static or moving platform with greater speed and accuracy than a 
trained shooter in the same scenario including counter-UAS kinetic defeat.
We are platform and payload agnostic and focus our development on the precision targeting hardware and software and on 
integrating solutions for our customers. When the AimLock auto-targeting capability is powered on, the system instantly begins 
detecting and tracking the desired target profiles, continuously re-aiming the weapon to maintain ideal shot placement on the target.
Additionally, the presence of multiple AimLock products in an operational scenario enables target handoff from one AimLock system - 
a remote weapons station on a ground vehicle for example - to another AimLock system like an individual weapon system in the door 
of a helicopter to increase battlefield situational awareness for all users enabled by our CTM autonomy nodes.
