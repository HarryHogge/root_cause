## Description

After VXE 635 was upgrade to 6.7.1 firmware and after power cycling after updating the VTOL Controller Firmware, the aircraft would connect in VXE SUAV, but would not detected the Centre Wing (CW).  The red light on the top of the CW would not light up either.  We tried connecting a different CW to VXE 635 and it connected as per normal.  We trying 635 on different fuselages and it could not be detected.
We were also not able to ping .10 address.  
Aircraft is being RMA to HSV
