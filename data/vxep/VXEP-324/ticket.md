## Description

In a few instances, operator nudge commands can be input when the vehicle is far from its desired setpoint.  Because the nudge inputs shift the north/east location of the desired TDP, this can result in undesirable shifts in the target TDP when the vehicle finally returns to the target point.  It is desirable to provide the operator with feedback to ensure they are aware of shifts that have been made to the target TDP.  See https://jenaero.atlassian.net/browse/VXEP-315 for details.
