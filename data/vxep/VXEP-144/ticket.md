## Description

The manual should be followed to prevent hot-swapping of lift booms during assembly.

"Hot swapping" or swapping lift components while the battery is connected to the aircraft is a practice that is not acceptable per the vehicle manual. 
Electrical Engineering confirmed that although the vehicle was designed to tolerate that action the mechanism to allow it does not work well, so the aircraft manual was updated this year to explicitly instruct not to do that or else this sparking can happen.
