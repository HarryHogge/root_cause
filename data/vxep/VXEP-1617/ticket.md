## Description

AC crashed during VTOL landing. Engineering is investigating and will give a dispo for each part of the AC once done. Description below
AC 491 (with customer CW 4036) had an incident today in completing flight number 1 at Min TOW.
AC was flying 6.5.0 FW
Weather: 10mph from S 248 72 deg F
Prior to launch, AC experienced issues establishing SA Camera feed through Media Client sw, ADP was swapped, but issue continued despite numerous attempts at restarting sw,and power cycling vehicle. Eventually decided to proceed using VLC media player for SA Camera feed. No issues were present during flight, vehicle successfully transitioned to VTOL and began descending normally, then, at approx 230ft AGL I lost SA Camera Feed and ground crew (Artis B.) reported the VTOL system had failed, causing AC to crash into the ground.
