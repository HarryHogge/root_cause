## Slide 3

Operator Report
Who:	USSS
What:	Stalker VXE30 (Tail #897) impacted obstacles and terrain on landing
Where:	Undesignated Location
When:	22 January 2025
Why:	During normal operation, tail #897 was commanded to “Land Now”. After transitioning to VTOL, the aircraft impacted obstacles and terrain below its position when attempting to land. 
Note: 	Root cause and contributing factors on next page.

## Slide 4

Executive Summary
Problem Statement​
VXE-897 was commanded to land, transitioned to VTOL, and subsequently impacted terrain and obstacles below after “falling” from its hover position.  An investigation was commenced to determine the root cause or causes of the impact.
Probable Sequence of Events 
The Operator selected “Land Now” from the Ground Control Station (GCS), possibly instead of “Return to Base”.  
The aircraft correctly transitioned to VTOL mode and began its decent. 
The operator then paused the descent in an effort to move the aircraft’s position, presumably to avoid ground obstacles.
The operator used the manual-nudge command (micro-nudge), which resulted in the aircraft not translating position as quickly as expected.
During the operator’s attempt to reposition the aircraft, the Hybrid Lift Battery was exhausted, and the aircraft could no longer maintain flight. 
The aircraft then descended uncontrollably, impacting a vehicle, then the terrain below. 
Root Cause ​
The operator’s decision to select “Land Now” instead of “Return to Base”, the subsequent decision to pause the aircraft’s decent, and the use of the wrong repositioning command caused the VTOL flight time to exceed the battery’s capacity, leading to loss of lift power and the subsequent crash.
Ruled out Leading Causes​
VTOL autopilot, VTOL Motors, Electronic Speed Controllers (ESC’s), and the HLB were all operating within design specifications. ​
Corrective Actions​
Suggestion to refresh operators on the differences between landing commands, manual position commands, and energy management, especially when manually operating the aircraft in VTOL mode.
