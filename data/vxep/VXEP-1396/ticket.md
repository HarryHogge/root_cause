## Description

We conducted a flight test of aircraft 674 today and found the fuel cell D4599988 to be running "hot".
 
As soon as it got to temp, it stayed maxed out on the temp gauge in the red. It did not indicate any cooling to the Green or yellow so we landed asap to prevent any damage.
During the landing logic, the temp gauge went into the yellow but climbed back to the red as soon as we were on the ground.
 
Temp cooled when we shut down the fuel cell, but we had an error and it never indicated that it had shut down.
