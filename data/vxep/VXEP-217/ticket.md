## Description

We currently have spare lift motor & esc’s in the catalog and when shipping them we have used either bubble wrap or the foam holders used in TCI kits to protect them. 
We would like to define that the correct thing is to use the foam holders. 
This may also be an opportunity to define a catalog PN versus internal PN since these are a catalog item.
