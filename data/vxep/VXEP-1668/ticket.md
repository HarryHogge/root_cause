## Description

“We had the Stalker connected and working the other day but after we came back in the next day and plugged everything back in, we are now running into an issue with the GCS connecting to the bird. The interesting part is the radios see each other and can confirm that with the Silvus radio page. When trying to connect the GCS knows what the tail number of the aircraft is and tries to connect but fails. What we have tried:
- Numerous restarts of the bird, radio, and GCS
- Changed the ground radios out for the other one in the kit
- Direct connected to the maintenance port
- Checked for additional instances of the program running

There was no change to the bird that we know of between when it was working and we powered own till when we powered back up and the GCS wouldn't connect.”
(Mike Mohr) We had them log into the Xcomm web page and check the throughput page. No AP data so we suspect a bad autopilot board. Both SD cards were replaced January of 2025.
