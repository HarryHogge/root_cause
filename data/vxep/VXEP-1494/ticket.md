## Description

| Traceability of delivered components |  | Delivered components did not have reviewed and released documentation or logs for drawings, build information, acceptance tests, travelers, QC inspections, etc. This limited ability to optimize the design for reliability, or know what parts were delivered in what configuration and what testing had been performed on each |

I think this can be one of our C/CAs that we can improve with process. What do you recommend for ensuring that all parts that are delivered to customers have an appropriate level of inspection and traceability? I think in SLO such parts still go through QC inspection to verify tracking and design documentation prior to delivery.  I can open a JIRA task to track this soon
