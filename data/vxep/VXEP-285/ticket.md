## Description

A development effort to create a stronger 18x9 blade was led in tangent to upgrading to the three blade propeller configuration. This blade was developed by Aero-Naut (Edge IRAD) and 30 samples were received for evaluation.
The three blade configuration provides a satisfactory solution to the propeller blade issue that led to AD# 2021-07-16.
Validating the stronger propeller blade may allow for increased capability and extended service life but is a relatively low priority item since the bulk of the problem has been solved.
