## Description

Description:
On take off the aircraft climbed like it normally would in some wind to about 80 ft. Once reaching that the aircraft then started to slow its accent and eventually got caught at 100-120ish feet. the aircraft motors seemed to increase throttle to what sounded to be full throttle the aircraft did not climb for a good 10 seconds or so while at the full throttle. Eventually 2 of the launches got past and continued the climb normally. one of the AC aborted and came down due to an error of "VTOL Low climb rate"
 
Winds on the ground 8-12 knots 
However winds aloft much closer to 20knots. We are on the face of a hill where the wind is coming up the hill 

DA- 2200ft

Files attached are all from same day of flight with similar conditions. 3 have the issue and 2 do not, noted in the name of the file
