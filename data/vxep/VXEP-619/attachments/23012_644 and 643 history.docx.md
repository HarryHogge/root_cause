Tail 644:

3 Edge SCO flight, 4 April, 2024

1 LM flight, 3 July, 2024, AAR reported 3 launch, 32 minutes

Preflight ground tests were completed on September 4th, 2024.

On September 5th, 2024, vehicle was flying at Santa Margarita Ranch with HD40 EO Trillium.

Approximately 2hour and 52 minutes into the flight, the vehicle rolled right, descended, and impacted the terrain. Level 3 damage.

644 Post Incident:

Avionics was installed in 643 and powered up. Successful connection to fixed wing autopilot and data files collected.

VTOL controller is damaged, can not recover VTOL log files using standard methods.

Preliminary FDR findings:

Event starts at 10295

Pitch rate is reported -1000 degree/sec, then back to normal levels, then back to -1000 degree/sec. *

For TDK MPU-9250, gyro full scale range is +/- 1000

Roll and Yaw rates are up to 70 degree/sec

Accelerations are reported in Az direction +/-1 g

Vehicle impacts terrain at time 10300 with airspeed of 135 ft/s

SA camera video shows the vehicle is in controlled orbit, then appears to look like it go over a speed bump, then rolls right, and impacts terrain. The shadow prior to impact shows the vehicle is intact.
