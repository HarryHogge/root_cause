## Description

This ticket addresses various changes to purchased components in the Dual SA Camera Assy. New PNs in bold
- Boson PCB kit was purchased as 2120695-01, is now 2125208-01 and consists of:
- Boson adapter, processor PCB was 2120696-01 and processed to 2120696-01-z, is now purchased as 2125084-01 (no cut traces needed) NEEDS DWG CHANGE
- Boson adapter, Camera PCB 2120695-01 is now coming with Conformal Coating (no CC = 2125218-01)
- Ribbon Cable (2121885-03) unchanged
- 2-Pin Picoblade Cable (2121905-01) unchanged
- Nylon Spacers, 2.2MM ID (2123734-01) unchanged
- Mipi Adapter PCB 2121744-01 is now coming with Conformal Coating (no CC = 2125219-01)
- Ribbon Cable, Processor Side, Mipi Adapter was 2121885-02 is now 2125160-01 from Axon NEEDS DWG CHANGE
- 1750 OEM PCB stack was 2 separate boards 2120624-01 and 2120703-01, is now purchased together and coated as just 2120624-01 (oreo) (no CC = 2125221-01)
- Omron camera comes with the ribbon cable as a kit. No separate part number for the cable required. Remove 2121885-01 and update the description for 2121009-01 to include Omron Camera plus ribbon cable. NEEDS DWG CHANGE
- 100-103480 blue CA should be replaced with dark pink 2135176-01
