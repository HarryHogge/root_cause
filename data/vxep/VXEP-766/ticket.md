## Description

For LRR competition, desire to have it fit in the back of a JLTV (cargo config). There is also a desire to fit into the UK Boxer vehicle. Target dimensions are unknown, but cases that fit into a 6x2x2 rectangle, and the fewest number, are desired.
Assume less equipment (e.g. do not put as much support equipment in, assume things like tools can be omitted if they take up significant space. Likely needs to include the SGCS2 and battery charging systems.
Initial phase will be to just create a prototype set for demo purposes, but overall project is Tier 3 since it would be a different product config.
Due date, assuming resourced, is March 30, 2025 - in time for LRR first delivery.
