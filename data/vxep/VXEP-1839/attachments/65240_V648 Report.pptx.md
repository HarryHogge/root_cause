## Slide 3

Operator Report
Who:	Lockhead Martin and OEM
What:	Stalker VXE30 Pre-MAGNSS Tail 648 experienced instances of the operator being unable to connect
Where:	Location undisclosed
When: 	21 April 2026
Why:	During the aircrafts’ boot sequence, the MAX305, a CAN transceiver that is on the XCOMM, failed to properly send CAN messages

## Slide 4

Executive Summary
Problem Statement​
During power-up, VXE 648 would not connect with the GCS.
​Sequence of Events per Engineering
Aircraft receives power and directs power to the XCOM
XCOM Microcontroller boots and CAN transceiver receives power
Damaged CAN transceiver fails to output message
Ruled out Leading Causes​
The center wing was initially investigated. However, once the center wing successfully connected to a different avionics box, the investigation shifted to the avionics box and its internals.
Root Cause ​
The MAX305 CAN transceiver was damaged.
Corrective Actions​
Aircraft received a new avionics box.
