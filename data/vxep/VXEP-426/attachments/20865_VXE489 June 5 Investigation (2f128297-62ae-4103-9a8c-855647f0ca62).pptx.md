## Slide 4

Incident Summary
Reporter LM FSR
Severity:
Comments
Edge Autonomy Proprietary Information
On two successive flights, following the landing transition, the aircraft swapped directions, rapidly lost altitude, and then recovered to a nominal landing. Constraints of the landing zone necessitated landing with a tailwind so weathervaning was expected. Winds on 5 June were 2.5 mph. The aircraft’s behavior was the same even with lighter winds. Would like an analysis of this behavior to determine if the aircraft is safe to continue flight operations.
Winds were not a factor for this incident.
