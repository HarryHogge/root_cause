## Description

VXE30+ HLB performance requirements.pptx
Test plan proposed (suggestions welcome)
- Measure solder joint temp of AS150 connectors built like the CW power connector.
- Connect to electrical load. Measure 150A for max takeoff duration seen in Cody’s analysis. https://jenaero.atlassian.net/browse/ENG-1695
- Repeat for 160A, 170A, 180, 190, and 200. End test before solder melting point.
- Repeat steps 1-3 for the following. 
- through current sense board. (input full current, each EC5 should experience~half the total current).
- Through EC5 boom connectors.
- Half the EC5 current through the EC3 ESC connectors.
- Repeat for 1/4 total current through the lift ESC. Identify hotspots and probable limits. Mineral bath and/or dyno  could be useful test methods.
