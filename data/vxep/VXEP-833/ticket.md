## Description

The scope of the TO2 MIL-STD-810H environmental testing covers temperature, shock, humidity, solar radiation, dust, sand, and rain testing. Two (2) separate test facilities were selected for the environmental testing: Experior, located in Oxnard, CA, and Element, located in Huntsville, AL. The two (2) test facilities were selected based on several factors including availability, cost, test chamber size, and capability to conduct the requested environmental testing.
