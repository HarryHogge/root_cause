Conclusion, for this proof of concept:
The sealant gets pushed inside the connector when placing the 
gasket back in place. It will not stay below the gasket where it 
has been applied due to the lack off space for it.
We do not see the possibility to guarantee a proper sealing of the 
connector by using the suggested method.
