## Description

There have been recently Customer rejections for chips on both the Forward Propeller Blades and Lift Blades. There is no clear acceptance or allowance for chips on blades, so they continue to be replaced when there is damage.
Ticket created for Engineering & Team to determine if there is any allowance for damage to blades or not… or this might just be vendor or a handling issue that needs to be resolved.

Misc Notes from Matt P talking with Flight Test Team:
We have historically not checked blades closely after Flight Test. We just look for grossly broken/damaged blades if something happens during flight like a hard landing. We have not had issues with small defects on the blades causing any issues in SCO or Flight Test… so its not something we specifically check.
 
Artis did note that if the forward blades are a bit loose they can touch the ground when the aircraft is landing which might contribute to some of the damage.. but not likely since he has seen some where the blade dug in on landing and it was still totally fine.
Example:  Wobble 580.MP4 (video of wing tip wobble that happens to show blades touching)
 
If we were to develop criteria it will probably have to be broken up in terms of location on the blade and size:
- Leading Edge – Damage here likely from flight
- Trailing Edge – Damage here likely from handling
- Tip – Least concern if there is damage (most allowance)
- Root – Most concern if there is damage (smallest allowance)

 
Last note is that the trailing edges on both blade styles are very thin and sharp. Extremely likely the small chips are from blades running into one another during storage, handling, or even in the vehicle case cuz they are just stored loose inside the pocket.
