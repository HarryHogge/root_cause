## Description

HELI-COIL INSERT 18-8 Stainless Helical Insert 10-32 Right-Hand Thread, 0.190" Long
This is the current part, we have to drill and tap out the G10 hardpoints inserted during layup. We instead want to replace this with an SLS part with a heat set insert. This will cut down finishing time because techs will only have to clean the threads that are already there rather than drill and tap and install Helicoil. This will be a more rigid process as well because we are merely cleaning the threads that are already there. It will also allow us to not have to worry about mis-installed helicoils. 

For further information please speak with Andrew McIntyre or Chris Jones.
