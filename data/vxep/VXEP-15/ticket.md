## Description

During the process of FAT, an issue was identified with the conformal coating on some of the circuit boards. Whilst not identified as a flight issue, the full impact of the issue is not known. This could lead to intermittent connections with some of the components and could cause a critical failure.
8D Link: 8D #21 VTOL AP Board Assy Conformal Coat Intrusion.pptx

## Corrective Action

Edge has investigated the issue and determined that all boards delivered to date are acceptable as they have passed multiple layers of comprehensive tests.
Added improved inspection processes
Moving to external processing of conformal coating from board house
