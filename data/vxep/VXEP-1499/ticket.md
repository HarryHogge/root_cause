## Description

30JUL (Morning)- 3rd SFG
- Attempted a Fuel Cell Flight on Stalker 559 (Which we believe has not been flown since we received if back from Alabama)
- Aircraft did not transition to fixed wing flight after VTOL launch at/about 300’ AGL
- Ground Crew noticed a “weird sound” on the VTOL motors and called to abort the launch
- Upon landing the HLB began smoking heavily.
- From what we can tell, the HLB and Center wing are completely NMC as of now. The Fuel Tank and Regulator also appear to be damaged. Unsure of the extent of damage to the Fuel Cell, Fueselage, HD40, and cables within the fuselage.
- Attempted to pull the AAR/FDR after it cooled down, failed both times. But they are attached here for what it did pull. (250730_0900)
