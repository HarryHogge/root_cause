## Description

IPC-D-620: “Acetic acid cure RTV silicone sealants, adhesives, and coatings shall not [A1A2A3] be used in the assembly or manufacture of electrical wiring harnesses and cable assemblies or as environmental sealants for electrical enclosures(e.g., conduit, fittings, and boxes).” (pg. 12) The RTV used in our electronic assemblies and harnesses is corrosive to non plated wires and contacts. This may also be the cause for the SD card problems that we have been seeing. It may be beneficial to switch RTV sealants in our electronic assemblies.
