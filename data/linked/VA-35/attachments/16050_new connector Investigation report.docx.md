New Connector Investigation

A new set of connectors were obtained to conduct the same test as the 452 investigation. The purpose of the test is to see the rate the temperature increases over time at different gap lengths. The lengths are 0.075”, 0.1”, 0.15”, and 0.2”. To get these distances calipers were used. The connectors were also tapped to mimic vibration from the drone. The set up stayed the same, however, the connector was not connected to the current sense board.

Test 1:

The test was conducted with a 0.075” gap and a constant 200 A applied. From the graph we can see that the rate of change is 49 C°/min. Also there is a slight decrease in current when the connector is tapped.

Test 2:

The test was conducted with a 0.1” gap and a constant 200 A applied. From the graph we can see that the rate of change is about 52 C°/min. The rate increased from the previous test showing that increasing the gap does affect the rate of change. The decrease in current is clearer compared to the first test.

Test 3:

The connector was moved to 0.15” with a constant 200A applied. The rate of change increased to about 69 C°/min. The change in current due to the vibration is sightly hard to see in this data however it is still present.

Test 4:

The gap increased to 0.2” with 200A still applied. The rate of change slightly increased to 70 C°/min which is not much a difference to test 3. However, when tapping was applied the current drop is more noticeable.

Conclusion:

The results of the test shows that as the gap increases from the connector the rate of heat over time increases. Also, if there is a gap and vibration is present the current drops. This is true for all gap distances. It seems that the rate of change caps at about a 0.15” gap because the rate did not increase too much.
