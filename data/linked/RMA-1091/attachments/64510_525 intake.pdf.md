RMA Electronics Checks 
 
 The unit has sustained substantial physical damage, which has rendered it unsuitable for electronic and 
avionics evaluation by the RMA and intake team. As such, no diagnostic or functional testing of these systems 
has been performed at this stage. Further assessment of the electronic and avionics components will be 
conducted by the engineering team to determine the extent of internal damage and potential recoverability. 
 
 Assemble the fuselage with the optical payload (Select the installed optical payload)

 Install the hybrid lift battery and plug in the HLB P4 leg of the Branch Cable Assy 
 Set the power supply to 25V and set the current limit to 2A. Turn off before plugging into the vehicle.  
 Assemble the center wing and lift booms (make sure all connectors are seated properly) 
 Place the center wing and lift booms onto the fuselage and connect the main system harness, dual current sense

 Using the turret controller, verify all turret ordinals (pressing forward button makes turret point towards

 Turret pan and tilt work throughout complete range of motion

operational (aft fuselage, both sides).

operational.

Sensor Checks: 
Open the XComm webpage to complete the following: 
 Go to Remote Air Data page (lower right of page).  Perform altimeter check using Test Cube. Draw syringe at
~5ml/s. Altitude should increase >50ft, Airspeed should increase >50fps. Press syringe, verify altitude and
airspeed decrease.
Caution: Excessive pressure or draw can damage the sensors. Do not exceed 200fps altitude variance during test
process.
 Perform airspeed check by pressurizing and holding total pressure tube and drain with moist finger.  Using the
Remote Air Data webpage, check that leak-down rate is less than 7ft/sec in 5 seconds.
 Check HLB communication in Battery page. Verify S/N matches.
Use GCS to review the following:
 Check gyros deg/sec direction and scale (right roll +, nose yaw right +, nose pitch up +) (found in Sensor Status)
o Limits: 0 +/- .1 deg/sec; If different record value here: _________________________
 Check accelerometers attitude (deg) estimate (nose up+, right roll+) in the instrument panel
o Limits: Pitch “reasonable: 0 +/- 4 degrees” and stable within 1 degree
o Limtis: Roll “reasonable: 0 +/- 5 degrees” and stable within 1 degree
Start the Launch Wizard: 
 Check control surface alignment and slop when in “surface alignment” page of the Launch Wizard (Elevator,
Rudder, Flaps, and Ailerons).
 Verify no servo binding noise occurs on any surfaces
 Follow remaining launch wizards instructions through to second calibration steps then cancel out of Launch
Wizard.
