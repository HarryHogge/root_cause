1. APPLICATION:  
This engineering supplement is applicable to all VXE30 systems and is to be used in conjunction 
with a XE25 Main Battery (P/N: 965-1-01-1836). 
 
2. PURPOSE: 
 
The purpose of this engineering supplement is to provide instruction on the installation of a 
XE25 battery into a VXE30 system as well as provide several operational considerations for 
flying with a XE25 battery. 
 
Installation of a XE25 battery reduces overall vehicle weight and increases payload capacity by 
about 3 lb. allowing 8.5 lb. of total payload capacity. Removal of the VXE30 battery has a 
significant effect on aircraft Center of Gravity (CG) and may require the Wing Shift Bracket (P/N: 
107102843) depending on the flight load out. 
 
Additional considerations when flying a XE25 battery include impacted flight endurance and 
incorrect endurance estimates. The XE25 battery will only provide half of the endurance 
limiting the VXE flight to about 2 hours in nominal conditions. It’s important to note that 
battery endurance estimates provided by the Ground Station Software will become inaccurate. 
Displayed voltage will provide a rough estimate of the system state, and while the system will 
still automatically trigger a Return to Base (RTB) command, it is recommended to build 
endurance margin into the flight plan and maintain a short distance to the LZ at all times. 
 
 
 
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
NOTE 
Missions with the aircraft flying extended distances are not recommended for aircraft in this 
configuration.

3. PROCEDURE: 
 
3.1 Secure a XE25 battery in the VXE30 Main FCR Battery Mount. The battery will be 
secured longitudinally like the VXE30 battery and strapped in the same way. The battery 
must be constrained to prevent movement laterally within the mount and should be 
roughly centered side to side to balance the weight. It is recommended to use two foam 
shims on either side of to the battery to achieve this. The foam shims shown below are 
1.9# EPP shims that measure 0.8” x 4.8”x 10” and can be provided upon request.  
 
 
Figure 2. XE25 Battery and Foam Shims 
         
Figure 3. XE25 Battery Installed in VXE30 System 
It is easiest to locate both foam shims in place, then slide the XE25 battery in between. 
 
NOTE 
The Hybrid Lift Battery will need to be removed to connect and disconnect the main power 
connector.

3.2 Perform a CG check on the aircraft using the CG tool. 
 
Ensure that the aircraft sits within the acceptable CG range of 4.2” to 5.3” in the desired 
flight loadout. The battery has a large effect on CG and the Wing Shift Bracket may need to 
be flown depending on the desired loadout.
