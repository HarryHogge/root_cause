Background: 
A small increase in MGTOW was recently introduced into the VXE30 Operations Manual (Rev 1.6) to 
account for increases in the airframe weight due to the addition of MOD Payload capabilities. To 
maintain thermal margin and control system authority, the available density altitude was also decreased 
for VTOL operation of the system.  
In addition, two engineering supplements have been created to allow customers, at their own risk, to 
increase the payload capacity of the VXE30 UAS. 
There are two methods for increasing available payload capacity in the system: 
• One method is to fly with an XE25 battery in place of the normal VXE30 battery per VXE-ES-23-
001 Increased VXE30 Payload Weight (XE25 Battery), Engineering Supplement. This method 
renders the GCS endurance estimates inaccurate and greatly reduces flight time. This is only 
recommended for localized missions. 
• The second method is to modify configuration values in the software such as airspeed setpoints, 
climb rate, and transition altitude per VXE-ES-23-002 Increased VXE30 Payload Weight (Modified 
Gains), Engineering Supplement. This allows for the system to be flown at a higher MGTOW; 
flying at a higher MGTOW will slightly reduce system endurance. This method also requires the 
VXE30 to transition from a lower altitude.
