## Description

This project governs the end-to-end development, release, validation, and delivery of Field Support Kits (FSKs) for the Octopus E140ZG2, E140MWIR, and E95 gimbals, including both Standard and LRR variants. The objective is to standardize the BOM structure, ensure drawing and part number traceability, and enable a quality-assured delivery process from Latvia to HSV to SLO.
The project spans coordination across Engineering, Operations, QA, and Program Management and includes legacy part reconciliation, new cable integration, and kit validation aligned with product requirements and field usability.

**PROJECT SUMMARY / PATH FORWARD**

- Finalize and release all BOMs and associated drawings
- Align part numbers between Riga and Edge BOMs
- Validate and integrate updated cables, desiccants, and kits
- Prepare HSV for receiving, inspecting, labeling, and shipping
- Track delivery flow from Latvia > HSV > SLO
- Ensure compatibility of voltage range with customer power supplies
