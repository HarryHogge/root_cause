## Description

Aircraft 422 on launch experienced the front right rotor locking up causing the aircraft to dive toward the front right. The rotor then restarted and caught itself and began to climb again. The same rotor then locked up a second time and we immediately landed the aircraft. During the execution if the immediate landing, the rotor started up and allowed it to land safely. After swapping both liftbooms we had a successful flight.
In addition, after all the issues we experienced today. We later went and gave the same bird without any modifications another try. Flight went off without a hitch. No idea why it didn't give us any problems this time but it was a successful flight resulting in 4 hours of flight time at a 55km distance.
Aircraft lost connection during swap from GCS2 to 3. Did not RTB
