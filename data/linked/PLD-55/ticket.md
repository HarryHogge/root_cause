## Description

Kit for E140 G2 and MWIR intended to be combined with the payload from Riga, in Huntsville, for direct shipment to customers.
To include:
- Interface PCB to allow safe and consistent interaction between E140 (G2 and MWIR) payload and VXE30 aircraft
- Enclosure for PCB to harden it to environmental conditions and electrically shielding the board
- Cable(s) to connect between the aircraft, interface board and payload.
- If required, a bracket geometry design, modified to mount the enclosure and manage cables

## Plan

Stage 1: Development
Prototyping and bench testing PCB architecture and functions. Fit check inside enclosure and kit components relative to one another and aircraft
Stage 2: LRIP - Order for Qty 4 MWIR needed urgently + dev/test units
Finalize design, complete bench testing and perform flight test.
Develop Tek Pubs documents for distribution to customers
Eng led build up of qty 4 kits for MWIR order to be delivered and installed by FSR(s)
Stage 3: Longer Term HSV Implementation
Production release of all engineering documents
Procure material to be assembled and kitted in HSV
Develop Production documentation for kit
