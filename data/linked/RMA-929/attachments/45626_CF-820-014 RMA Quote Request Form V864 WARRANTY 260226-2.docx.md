Directions: Use this form to request a quote for a Repair and Maintenance Authorization (RMA), including required materials and labor, for warranty and non-warranty repairs. This form is used to collect all information necessary from Global Support, Engineering, and Supply Chain regarding the customer, affected equipment, scope of work, and lead time, for the Quotes Team to generate an accurate RMA quote.

The Document Owner is the assigned Quote Lead. The designated Point of Contact (POC) from the requesting organization, typically Global Support, completes Section 1 to identify the affected equipment, contractual context, shipping and billing information, and required lead time. The POC completes Sections 2 and 3 to identify required replacement materials and labor estimates necessary to restore the equipment to operational condition. Use Section 4 to provide relevant notes to Production or other teams.

Upon submission, the Quote Lead reviews the form for completeness and accuracy before preparing the quote. Approved RMA quote details are used to generate the formal quote and, upon customer authorization, to initiate repair actions and material procurement.
