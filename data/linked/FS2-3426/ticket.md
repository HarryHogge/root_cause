### Ben Norris, 2025-06-25

We inspected the batteries and found the VXE30 FCR Main Battery to be leaking electrolyte with a shattered lid but no obvious damage to the wiring, a battery checker was used to confirm state of charge to be approximately ~84% with an imbalance of ~23mV between cells. 
The VXE30 HLB also had a shattered lid and cracked case, it did not seem to have any leaks but was warm to the touch and measured above 30° when inspected with the FLiR infrared camera. It also was rising in temperature as we were inspecting it. We also measure the voltage with a battery checker and it was around ~40% state of charge with an approximate ~21mV imbalance. 
At the time I did not feel comfortable just storing these packs overnight so we made the decision to use a baking soda and water bath to facilitate discharge and prevent fire spread.
