We continue to review data on the incident with VXE401. In the interest of insuring the most favorable outcome of future flights, we are advising against any further flights outside of the published limitations for MTOW of the VXE30. 

MTOW battery config is 48.0lb / 21.8kg

MTOW fuel cell config is 44.0lb / 20.0kg

We are looking into the possibility that the higher weight takeoffs are exceeding our systems design limitations for max current of the VTOL components. 

Additionally, it is requested that both batteries related to the incidents be returned to San Luis Obispo for further analysis.
