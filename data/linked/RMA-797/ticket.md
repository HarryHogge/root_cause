## Description

| When launching the Stalker VXE-30, the aircraft got to its transition altitude. When it began to transition, the motors started making a “weird” audible noise noticed by the ground crew. The aircraft also was not moving forward during this time. The ground crew made the call to abort the launch, which the Vehicle Operator (VO) selected. At that time, the Stalker descended to the ground and landed. When it landed the motors did not cut off automatically and had to be manually disabled, which is when the Hybrid Lift Battery (HLB) audibly “popped” and then immediately started smoking.  We let it cool down for a couple hours and then removed the components that did not seem affected. |
