## Page 9

Conclusion
• OAT in flight data reads 38.6
• Slide 3 shows divergence in IAS (indicated airspeed) from the pitot static system and airspeed as measured by the GPS. The 
data indicates the operation of the pitot static system degrades over time.
• Slide 4 shows throttle setting and trim going to 100%
• Slide 5 suggests aircraft was operated in weather conditions below 32°F in precipitation
• Failure symptoms could not be replicated on the ADP assembly
• ADP 0071 was flown on aircraft VXE371 multiple times without incident
• Probable causes of pitot static systems can be weather related when snow or freezing rain blocks the pitot tube. This 
causes airspeed to decay and prevents safe flight.
