### Jack Waeschle, 2025-05-13

@Drew Hildreth @Rodney Linch @Eric Rinell FYSA
Composite repair dispos:
For the bulkhead repair: Trim loose or damaged fibers. Clean with isopropyl. Sand area to abrade surface. Clean with isopropyl. Wet layup repair with MGS resin with 3 wraps of uni extending at least 1 inch past damage, then 1 ply wrap of 3k oriented at 45 degrees. Wrap repair plies around bulkhead flange such that each ply adds material to the inside and outside of the bulkhead flange. Provide compaction if possible. Sand wire pass through and any sharp spots of resin as needed post cure.
For the cracks in fuselage skin: Clean area with isopropyl. Sand with 220 grit. Clean with isopropyl. MGS resin wet layup repair with 2 plies of 3k Carbon fabric. Ensure patches extend at least 1 inch past cracks/damage. Permissible to stagger sizing such that there is a smoother transition in ply thickness in patch area.
