## Page 5

5
Investigation Status/Known Conclusions
1. In a standard VXE aircraft. The load difference from 0 throttle vs 100% with no propeller is ~30W. Since there is no corresponding power change with throttle changes after the sudden loss of 
propulsion, this indicates that the motor is not spinning. 
2. Since power and other data was recorded and communicated after the loss of propulsion, this indicates that power was present at least to the current sense board in the nose. This indicates a likely 
failure of harness between the CSB and ESC, the throttle signal harness, or something in the ESC assembly.

Edge Autonomy Proprietary Information
Aircraft experienced loss of altitude control over 
target objective in orbit 4. Operator re-
commanded orbit 4 however the aircraft was 
unable to maintain altitude. Orbit 3 was 
commanded to fly the aircraft away from 
objective. Aircraft was still unable to maintain 
altitude and U-drive was commanded to attempt 
to regain altitude control and manually steer the 
aircraft away. Link was lost at 367 feet before the 
U-drive command could be sent. MSOC declined 
to recover aircraft due to partner force protection 
and operational concerns.
• Aircraft was in Orbit
• Aircraft was unable to maintain altitude
• Aircraft was commanded to fly away from objective
      (new orbit)
• Aircraft was unable to maintain altitude
• U-drive was attempted and failed
• Communications was lost with aircraft
