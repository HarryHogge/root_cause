## Description

In Summer 2025 multiple occurrences of Hybrid Lift Battery Cell Failure led to the failed launch of aircraft in the field. These failures have caused fleetwide concerns to the health/functionality of HLBs for VXE30 aircraft and beyond.
This item is to address the development of a tool for field checking and validating batteries, as well as root cause corrective actions to address the issue at a systematic level.

## Plan

Development of a field use tool to be brought into the field to test battery performance and offer replacements for any that show evidence of damage or poor performance.
- Customer Notification of issue that includes advice on storage and maintenance of batteries.
- Offer to perform health checks with provided tool
- Continued study of the dropout and reasons for occurrence.
