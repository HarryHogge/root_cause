On 30 October 2024 the 3rd SFG(A) conducted an operational check on aircraft using different main battery
than what flew on the 12 October flight. Three minutes after the aircraft was powered, a strong burning smell
came from the inside of the aircraft. Main power cable plugs in the main battery had a corrosive substance
on it. While deployed, 3SFG operators made a point to ensure these ports did not have carbon buildup and
verified the port was clean prior to use. Unit operators believe the issue is what contributed to the rapid
battery fluctuation during the 12 October flight.

On 12OCT2024 at roughly 1710L // Battery passed all its pre-launch checks. It also passed its battery health
check after the flight per the maintenance manual. After conducting an RTB at 32% the aircraft main battery
percentage started fluctuating from 16-30% rapidly. This started after beginning IP 1 and didn’t end until
passing into IP4. The aircraft landed safely and the VXE software reported 30% main battery life on ground.
After allowing the battery to cool down, the main battery charger reported battery at 12%.
