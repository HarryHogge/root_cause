## Description

- On 30 OCT 2024, 3rd SFG(A) conducted an operational check on AC using different main batteries that flew on the 12 OCT 24 flight. Three minutes after AC was powered, a strong burning smell came from within the AC. The main power cable plugs in the MB had a corrosive substance on it. While deployed, 3SFG operators made a point to ensure these ports did not have carbon buildup and verified port was clean prior to use. Unit operators believe the issue is what contributed to the rapid battery fluctuation during the 12 OCT 24 flight.
- On 12 OCT 24 at roughly 1710L the battery passed all its pre-launch checks. It also passed its battery health check after the first flight per the maintenance manual. After conducting an RTB at 32%, the AC MB% started fluctuating from 16-30% rapidly. This started after beginning IP 1 and didn’t end until passing into IP 4. The AC landed safely, and the VXE software reported 30% main battery life on ground. After allowing the battery to cool down, the MB charger reported the battery at 12%.
