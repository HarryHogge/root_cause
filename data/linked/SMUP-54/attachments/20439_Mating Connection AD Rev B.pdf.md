Overview 
 
Purpose:
Procedural improvements have been developed to identify and prevent potential powerconnection failures. New procedures will help keep the black and orange AS150 connectorsfully seated in both the Hybrid Lift Battery (HLB) and Main Battery. New inspections will providevisibiltity and guidance for flight critical preventative maintenance. The following documentaddresses the newly created inspections and maintenance procedures which can mitigatedamage to the aircraft.
 
Scope:
Airworthiness Directive (AD)_Mating Connection_2024-05-24_B applies to all Operators andTechnicians of the VXE30 Unmanned Aircraft System.
 
Compliance:
If listed inspections and maintenance procedures are not followed, loss of aircraft could occur.

Metal Snap Rings, Inspect  
Missing metal snap rings are critical to hold aircraft AS150 power connection pins in place. This inspection
is required for every pre-flight and post-flight.
Steps:
CAUTION
Risk of flight safety occurs if utilizing battery with one or both metal snap rings missing.
 
1. Verify metal snap rings are present on all receptacles for black and orange AS150 connectors.
 
2. If one or both metal snap rings are not present, do not use components. Report finding and RMA part number,
if found.

Solder Melt & Pin/Connector Damage, Inspect
If the black, AS150 center wing connector is not fully seated in the receiving HLB connector, overheating will occur
leading to solder and pin damage. Sparking damage to orange AS150 connectors may also be present if the
published aircraft assembly/ disassembly procedure is not followed. This inspection is required for every pre-flight
and post-flight.
Steps:
CAUTION
Risk of flight safety occurs if utilizing battery with solder or sparking damage present.
 
1. Verify no damage is present to connectors and pins from all black and orange AS150 plugs. 
 
2. If solder or sparking damage is present, do not use component. Report finding and RMA part number.

Power Contacts for FOD and Corrosion, Inspect and Remove
Foreign Object Debris (FOD) residing on power connections can cause corrosion and significantly increase the
contact resistance, which can lead to excessive heat and failure. Carbon deposits may also develop insdie
connectors if power is applied to with FOD present. Inspect all AS150 connectors to verify free and clear of FOD.
This inspection is required for every pre-flight.
1. Preparation for Task:
Required Tools:
Tools Part Number
HLB Mate Indicator Kit 107-114495
Foam Swab 100-114548
 
Required Parts:
Parts Part Number
Isopropyl Alcohol N/A
 
2. Procedure:
2.1: Verify all AS150 connector pins and receptacles are free of FOD and corrosion. If corrosion is present, do not
use component. Report finding and RMA part number
 
2.2: If carbon deposits and/or FOD are discovered, remove using Isopropyl alcohol and Foam Swab (P/N 100-
114548). 
NOTE
The black carbon deposits can be removed with Isopropyl alchohol. If deposits aren’t removed after cleaning, the
connector is most likely damaged due to contact sparking.

O-Ring Lubrication, Inspect and Apply
The O-ring located around black and orange AS150 connectors now has a maintenance procedure and requires an
inspection every preflight. Silicone grease utilized to keep HLB and Main battery connections smooth and secure
may dry out due to frequent use. This creates difficulty fully connecting and seating the batteries.
1. Preparation for Task:
Required Tools:
Tools Part Number
HLB Mate Indicator Kit 107-114495
Foam Swab 100-114548
 
Required Parts:
Parts Part Number
Silicone Grease 100-102819
 
     2. Procedure:
2.1: Verify O-ring around black and orange AS150 connectors is lubricated with silicone grease.
2.2: If the O-Ring is dry, coat the O-Ring with a small amount of silicone grease (P/N 100-102819). Use the foam
swabs (P/N 100-114548) for application of the grease located in the HLB Mate Indicator Kit (P/N 107-114495).
Repeat step for the other connector, if applicable.

HLB Mate Indicator Installation
It was noted in a series of test flights that certain AS150 connectors were prevented from fully mating. A mate
indicator label will now be added to the black AS150 center wing connector to alert the operator of this issue before
damage can occur during flight. The Operator/Technician will now require an HLB Mate Indicator Kit containing a
HLB Mate Indicator Label for the black AS150 VXE30 Center Wing Assembly connector.
1. Preparation for Task:
Required Tools:
Tools Part Number
N/A N/A
 
Required Parts:
Parts Part Number
Center Wing Panel Assy 107-100455
Hybrid Lift Battery 107-101191
Hybrid Lift Battery, AWX 114-106085
HLB Mate Indicator Label 107-114271
HLB Mate Indicator Kit 107-114495
 
      2. Procedure:
2.1: Verify all adhesive is removed from the black and orange AS150 connectors within the batteries. If any is
present, follow the adhesive “squeeze-out” removal maintenance procedure.
2.2: Locate the HLB Mate Indicator Label (P/N 107-114271) within the HLB Mate Indicator Kit (P/N 107-114495).
2.3: Start the application of the label in the middle of the diagonal plane on the black AS150 center wing connector.
The red edge should face the connector opening, the black edge should be flush with the backwall. The red
curvature should wrap around one of the curves on the connector. After finishing the wrap, the label should finish
close to the starting point. A small gap between start and finish is normal.
