## Overview

## Purpose:

Procedural improvements have been developed to identify and prevent potential power connection failures. FOD on the power contacts can significantly increase the contact resistance, which can lead to excessive heat and failure. The following document addresses the newly created inspection procedures which can mitigate high resistance contacts.

## Scope:

Section 5.1 Contact inspection and cleaning if required should be performed ASAP for each vehicle, and before and after each flight.

Section 5.2 applies to all VXE30 Center Wing Assemblies with a serial number <550. This can be performed at Edge Autonomy or by an Edge Autonomy Field Service Representative.

## Compliance:

If listed inspections and maintenance procedures are not followed, loss of aircraft could occur.

## Cross Reference:

Airworthiness Directive (AD)_ Mating Connection_2023-10-12

## Inspections

## Contact Inspection

Ensure connectors are inspected per Section 2.2 Solder Melt/ Pin Damage, Inspect of Airworthiness Directive (AD)_ Mating Connection_2023-10-12 before and after each flight.

Inspect both sets of battery connector plugs and receptacles for FOD. If present, thoroughly clean contacts with a Q-Tip and isopropyl alcohol.
