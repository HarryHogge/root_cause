Test setup
Output voltage of the power supply was adjusted to 12.0V by holding start/enter key until ‘beep’ is heard. Using left/right arrows, voltage was adjusted to 12.0V.
Enter key was then selected again to power output on.
[picture]
Battery charger was connected to battery. Upon selecting charge, battery charger screen indicated “input voltage lower than 12.6V!” 

Adjusted input voltage (output of power supply) to >12.6V cleared the problem.
