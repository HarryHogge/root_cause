 965F0001-115 STALKER VXE30 SYS 
BASIC 
V547, V533 We were flying it in Fuel Cell configuration and had a 
failure on launch. The weight was at 40 pounds and CG 
was between 4.7and 4.8. We conducted preflight 
through the launch wizard and everything was 
functional and within tolerance. All of the motor 
functions, battery levels, and power draws were within 
tolerance per the manual. The fuel cell was generating 
power and had prompted the HLB was sufficient for 
take-off. Upon reaching the 20ft altitude level all system 
functions appeared normal. once the aircraft gained 
elevation from 20ft agl i began to notice a cell 
imbalance in the VTOL control window and the lift 
motors gave an audible struggle. I also could see 
visually that the lift motors were not functioning 
properly. After a command ABORT Launch and the 
vehicle responded with an RTL command in the VTOL 
control window. The vehicle struggled through descent 
and drifted about 50 ft back into the transportation 
boxes. The left lift boom snapped in half and several lift 
motor propellors broke but these were the only observed 
damages at this time. V533- Was using battery. CG was 
tested and within limits. weight was within tolerance. It 
took off and VTOL was normal. upon transition it didn't 
fully switch. There was no forward prop spin. The 
Battery level was at 70 percent.

• Fill out all sections with the requested information. Please ensure that all 
contact information is accurate for ease of communication. 
 
• In the “Equipment Information” section, please describe the issues you are 
experiencing. Please be as detailed as possible. This information will assist our 
team during the evaluation process. A member of Redwire Defense Tech may 
reach out to discuss issues further.  
 
• Once Redwire Defense Tech receives your RMA request you will be notified Via 
email and a representative from our support team will reach out to coordinate 
the next step in the process. 
 
• Please ship ALL equipment in the original transit cases to ensure the 
equipment makes it safe to and from Redwire Defense Tech. 
 
• Once the equipment is received, Redwire Defense Tech will conduct an
