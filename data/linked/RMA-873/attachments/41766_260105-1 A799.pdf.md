RMA Instructions  
• Fill out all sections with the requested information. Please ensure that all 
contact information is accurate for ease of communication.  
  
• In the “Equipment Information” section, please describe the issues you are 
experiencing. Please be as detailed as possible. This information will assist our 
team during the evaluation process. A member from Edge Autonomy may 
reach out to discuss issues further.   
  
• Once Edge Autonomy receives your RMA request you will be notified Via email 
and a representative from our support team will reach out to coordinate the 
next step in the process.  
  
• Please ship ALL equipment in the original transit cases to ensure the 
equipment makes it safely to and from Edge Autonomy.  
  
• Once the equipment is received, Edge Autonomy will conduct an inventory and 
receiving inspection. We will compare it to what you requested and contact you 
if any discrepancies exist. We will send you our initial findings and notify you 
when we have started the evaluation. Once a repair quote is generated, we will 
notify you of warranty status.  Anything not covered by the warranty will be 
priced out, and a quote for repair will be provided with payment terms.  
  
• You, the customer, can choose to repair or have us return the equipment as 
Beyond Economic Repair (BER) if not covered under warranty.   
  
• Repaired or replacement equipment will be shipped to the return address 
provided in Customer shipping address portion of the RMA form. Please 
ensure that all information is accurate.
