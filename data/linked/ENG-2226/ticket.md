## Description

Corrective Action for 8D #24 - CW to HLB AS150 Connection Issue
Currently the drawing does not have a clear requirement to ensure no adhesive squeeze out is left on the mating surface of both of the AS150 connectors.
This has caused problems where the connection to the aircraft through the Dual Current Sense cable is not able to fully mate due to this squeeze-out.
Production Procedure and Inspection Procedure will be updated immediately, but the drawing should be updated to make this requirement clear as well especially since these batteries will be assembled in Ann Arbor soon.
