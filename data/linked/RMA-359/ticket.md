## Description

ERI said there is damage caused by an unintended landing of VXE-628.
Notes from ERI: Damaged during unintentional landing, case latches are bent and cannot be closed.

Engineering Evaluation and repair
Induction, inventory and basic evaluation found the entire aircraft was catastrophically damaged and replace ALL is the recommendation.

NOTE:  Aircraft fuselage and Nose cone was found to be VXE-628 and destroyed. 628 Center wing was being repaired when the customer decided to use 501 parts to fly VXE-628
