## Slide 4

Tear-Down Executive Summary
PROPRIETARY AND CONFIDENTIAL THE INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS DOCUMENT IS THE PROPERTY OF EDGE AUTONOMY. ANY REPRODUCTION IN PART OR AS A WHOLE WITHOUT THE WRITTEN PERMISSION OF EDGE AUTONOMY IS PROHIBITED.
Two AE7844152XT 4.4 Ah cells from Advance Energy were torn down to root cause the extreme voltage drop observed when tested under load.
Physical analysis of the internal components of 2-3785-2A and 2-4415-6B suggests the root cause of the poor electrical performance is inadequate electrolyte volume, causing high ohmic and concentration polarization.
It is currently suspected that the observed cell dryness is from a pouch sealing issue during cell manufacturing.
