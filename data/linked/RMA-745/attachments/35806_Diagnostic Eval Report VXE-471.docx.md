## 3. Summary of Findings

Service bulletins- Transit Case placards, Screws. RF Cables, SD Cards, Fuselage power Harness update

Repairs-  Composite Repairs, Power components must be replaced due to failure after modification.

## 4. Visual Inspection

Used Condition-  Dirty, Smoke smell coming from fuselage upon opening transit case. Crack in Fuselage payload opening, composites repair needed on Wing-tip (L) along with fairing and lift rotor blade (CW)

Missing- Fuselage Aft Port cover, Center Wing End Pocket Assy (L&R)

Modifications made- Tiedown for nosecone power Harness.

## 5. Functional Testing

Spark upon power up. Showing power fluctuations. Pulled Nose cone off to test branch cable components and found modified and melted main power harness. Tested the harness which resulted in finding a shortage. In turn, we found the Nose Power harness, Fuselage harness, and Avionics Box were all damaged beyond repair. Silvus radio was checked to be in working and there was no damage to auxiliary power line, thus saving the branch cable and fuse payload harness

## 6. Diagnostic Results

Due to condition of bird no further electronics evaluations were made. However, while testing the Silvus Radio, it was determined that it will also need a software update.
