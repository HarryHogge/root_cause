@Sean Odell why does the quote say full aircraft?  The damage in the pictures and the account of the mishap do not align with this.

### Sean Odell, 2024-07-10

@Pat Wells Multiple support fractures throughout plane. Not a lot of exterior fractures, but rather internal. Spar damage possible on both wing tips, not including the punctures and gashes. Fuse is NFF do to 2 of 3 ribs broken or fractured.  ONLY potential salvageable part is the tail boom. No HIPOT to guarantee the electronics are safe either. Therefore the safest choice for this aircraft is to replace.
