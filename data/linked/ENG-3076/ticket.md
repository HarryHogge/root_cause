## Description

MARSOC OCONUS Crash.
Stalker VXE30 was descending and began to drift from the desired land location. VO went to command land  immediately since user landing controls did not populate. Before VO could select land immediately the AV pointed downward and crashed nose first within 3 seconds.
This was the first time Stalker 425 was flown in country. Stalker 425 had 2 successful flights prior to this back in the US. During pre-flights, VO noticed that VTOL motors did not reach its usual test speed. VO then swapped out the HLB and re-tested. Second test was a success. AV was then launched and climbed to 1500 ft AGL with no issues. Once established at altitude for about 5 mins, AV began having degraded GPS issues, causing the AV to lose and regain GPS every couple of mins. VO would re-command it back to its orbit once GPS was regained and kept a close eye on it. After about an hour and no sign of it resolving, VO commanded it to land. Once at 800 ft AGL, AV had no more GPS issues showing 7-9 satellites locked. AV entered its VTOL landing at its set 200 ft AGL then VO saw as it began to descend down it began to turn and drift away from its desired location. User landing controls did not auto populate so VO went into the menu to selected VTOL control panel IOT select land immediately to give the VO controls to nudge the AV back to its desired location. Before the VO selected land immediately, the AV nose pitched down fast and hard crashing nose first into the ground.
