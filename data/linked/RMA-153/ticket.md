Aircraft arrived and initial evaluation complete. Please see attachments.

Aircraft intake complete and aircraft delivered to Engineering for your continued evaluation. Photos are on the RMA ticket linked.

### Sean Odell, 2023-12-08

Initial Evaluation showed the following:
Fuse/Nose: Replace
Vertical Tail: Replace
Horizontal Stab: Replace
Tail Boom: Replace
Center Wing: Replace
Left and Right Lift Boom: Replace Both
Left Wing Tip: minor creases, New Clip needed
Right Wing Tip: Minor Creases, New Clip Needed

### Cameron Chapman, 2023-12-12

@Sean Odell due to the severity of the impact. I recommend replacing everything other than the following 
- Cables. If no damage, ok to use in EA asset or customer with customer approval. Risk damage to solder joints, contacts, etc.
- Mechanical non-structural. If no damage, ok to return with engineering approval.
